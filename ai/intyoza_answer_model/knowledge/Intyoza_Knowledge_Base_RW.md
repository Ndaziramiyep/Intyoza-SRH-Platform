# Ububiko bw'Ubumenyi bwa Intyoza (Ikinyarwanda)

## Amakuru y'ingenzi ku buzima bw'imyororokere bw'urubyiruko mu Rwanda (yagenzuwe Ukwakira 2026)

**Amategeko**
- Itegeko N° 026/2025 rigenga serivisi z'ubuvuzi (ryatangiye gukurikizwa ku wa 18 Nzeri 2025): abafite imyaka 15 kuzamura bashobora kwiyemerera serivisi z'ubuzima, harimo kuboneza urubyaro, kwirinda virusi itera SIDA n'izindi serivisi z'imyororokere, batabanje gusaba uruhushya rw'ababyeyi cyangwa undi muntu.
- Itegeko N° 68/2018 (Igitabo cy'amategeko ahana), gusambanya umwana: igifungo cy'imyaka 20 kugeza kuri 25; igifungo cya burundu iyo umwana ari munsi y'imyaka 14. Imibonano hagati y'abana bafite imyaka 14 kuzamura nta gahato cyangwa iterabwoba ntihanwa.
- Gukuramo inda biremewe iyo uyitwite ari umwana, nyuma yo gufatwa ku ngufu, gushyingirwa ku gahato cyangwa isano ya bugufi, cyangwa iyo inda ishyira mu kaga ubuzima bw'umubyeyi cyangwa bw'umwana. Ntibisaba icyemezo cy'urukiko (kuva 2019). Iteka rya Minisitiri ryasohotse mu Ukuboza 2024 ryemerera ibigo nderabuzima n'amavuriro byemewe, ndetse n'ibitaro, kubitanga, akenshi mbere y'ibyumweru 22. Ku mwana, iyo ababyeyi n'umwana batumvikanye, icyifuzo cy'umwana ni cyo cyubahirizwa.
- Imyaka yemewe yo gushyingirwa: 21.

**Imibare**
- Ubushakashatsi DHS 2025: 8% by'abakobwa bafite imyaka 15 kugeza kuri 19 bigeze gutwita, bavuye kuri 5% muri 2019-20. Igipimo cy'uburumbuke 3.7 (2020: 4.1). Abipimishije inda nibura inshuro 4: 78% (2020: 47%). Abapfa babyara: 149 ku bana 100,000 bavutse ari bazima (2020: 203).
- Virusi itera SIDA: nka 1.2% by'abakobwa na 0.5% by'abahungu bafite imyaka 15 kugeza kuri 24; urubyiruko rubarirwamo nka kimwe cya gatatu cy'ubwandu bushya.
- Ibibazo by'ihohoterwa rishingiye ku gitsina byakiriwe byavuye ku 33,844 muri 2021 bigera ku 42,743 muri 2025.

**Serivisi**
- Isange One Stop Centre 48 zifatanye n'ibitaro by'uturere 37 n'ibitaro bikuru 10: ubuvuzi, ubujyanama, ubufasha bwa polisi n'ubw'amategeko ku buntu kandi mu ibanga.
- Serivisi z'urubyiruko (youth corners) ku bigo nderabuzima byinshi no mu bigo by'urubyiruko.
- Urukingo rwa HPV ku bakobwa bari mu kigero cy'amashuri (doze imwe); Gardasil 9 yatangiye gutangwa muri 2026 i Karongi, Nyarugenge, Kayonza na Gicumbi.

**Nimero z'ubufasha**: 112 (ibyihutirwa), 114 (umurongo w'ubuzima wa RBC, ni ubuntu), 3029 (Isange, ni ubuntu), 3512 (ihohoterwa), 116 (abana, ni ubuntu), 166 (RIB).

## Imihango n'ubwangavu (Menstruation and puberty)

### MEN-032: Ubwangavu n'ubugimbi ni iki?

Ubwangavu (ku bakobwa) n'ubugimbi (ku bahungu) ni igihe umubiri w'umwana uhinduka ukaba uw'umuntu mukuru. Akenshi butangira hagati y'imyaka 8 na 13 ku bakobwa, na hagati ya 9 na 14 ku bahungu. Imisemburo ituma umuntu akura vuba, akamera ubwoya mu maha no ku myanya ndangagitsina, akazana ibiheri n'ibyiyumvo bishya. Abakobwa bamera amabere bagatangira imihango; abahungu ijwi rirahinduka, igitsina n'amabya bigakura, kandi bashobora kurota basohora. Buri wese atangira igihe cye, kandi ibi byose ni ibisanzwe.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MEN-033: Uruhererekane rw'imihango ni iki, kandi ovulation ni iki?

Uruhererekane rw'imihango ni igikorwa kiba buri kwezi gitegura umubiri w'umugore ku buryo ashobora gusama. Rubarwa kuva ku munsi wa mbere w'imihango kugeza ku munsi wa mbere w'ikurikiyeho, akenshi iminsi 21 kugeza kuri 35. Hafi ya hagati y'uruhererekane, agasabo k'intanga karekura intanga ngore: ibyo ni ovulation, kandi iminsi iri hafi yaho ni yo umukobwa ashobora gusama cyane. Iyo intanga ngore idahuye n'intanga ngabo, igice cy'imbere cya nyababyeyi kirasohoka, bikaba imihango.

*risk: None; sources: MEN; PUB*

### MEN-034: Imyanya y'imyororokere ni iyihe?

Imyanya y'imyororokere ni ibice by'umubiri bikoreshwa mu kubyara. Iby'umukobwa birimo igitsina cyo hanze (vulva), n'imbere: igitsina (vagina), nyababyeyi aho umwana akurira, n'udusabo tw'intanga tubiri tubika intanga ngore. Iby'umuhungu birimo igitsina n'uruhu rw'amabya rubika amabya abiri akora intanga ngabo kuva mu bugimbi. Kumenya amazina nyayo bigufasha kwita ku mubiri wawe no gusobanurira umukozi w'ubuzima ikibazo ufite.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MEN-001: Imihango ni iki?

Imihango ni igihe nyababyeyi isohora amaraso n'utundi turemangingo binyuze mu gitsina. Ni ibisanzwe kandi ni ikimenyetso cy'uko umubiri ukura neza. Akenshi imara iminsi 2 kugeza kuri 5, ariko biratandukana ku muntu ku wundi.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-002: Nzajya mu mihango bwa mbere ryari?

Abakobwa benshi bajya mu mihango bwa mbere hagati y'imyaka 10 na 16. Buri mubiri ugira igihe cyawo. Imirire mibi, imyitozo ikomeye cyane cyangwa ibibazo bimwe by'ubuzima bishobora kubitinza. Niba bigutera impungenge, umukozi w'ubuzima yagusuzuma akareba ko byose bimeze neza.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko ku kigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-003: Imihango yanjye iza mu bihe bitandukanye buri kwezi. Hari ikibazo mfite?

Ukwezi k'imihango akenshi kumara iminsi 28, ariko gushobora kuba kugufi cyangwa kurekure, kandi ni ibisanzwe ko guhinduka mu myaka ya mbere nyuma yo gutangira imihango. Jya kwa muganga niba imihango ikomeje kuza nabi cyane, ikaza gake cyane, cyangwa ikamara igihe kirekire kurusha uko bisanzwe.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko ku kigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-004: Imihango yanjye ni myinshi cyane, nduzura cotex vuba kandi nkumva nzunguye. Ni byo?

Imihango myinshi cyane cyangwa imara igihe kirekire ni ikimenyetso cyo kujya kwisuzumisha. Ishobora guterwa n'imisemburo itaringaniye, kandi gutakaza amaraso menshi bishobora gutera kubura amaraso, bigatuma wumva unaniwe, ufite intege nke cyangwa uzungera. Jya kwa muganga vuba. Niba wumva ugiye guta ubwenge cyangwa amaraso ari menshi cyane, genda uyu munsi.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: MEN*

### MEN-005: Ni ibisanzwe kubabara cyane mu gihe cy'imihango?

Kuribwa mu nda mu gihe cy'imihango birasanzwe. Ariko ububabare ntibukwiye kukubuza kubaho. Niba bugutera gusiba ishuri cyangwa ibikorwa bya buri munsi, umukozi w'ubuzima yagufasha kumenya icyabuteye n'uburyo bwo kubworoshya.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-006: Nshobora koga cyangwa kwisukura ndi mu mihango?

Yego. Koga umubiri n'imyanya ndangagitsina ukoresheje amazi meza n'isabune mu gihe cy'imihango ni isuku nziza. Ibivugwa ko koga uri mu mihango ari bibi si ukuri, kandi kutisukura byongera ibyago byo kwandura indwara.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-007: Nshobora gukina siporo no kujya ku ishuri ndi mu mihango?

Yego. Ushobora gukora imyitozo, gukina, koga, gukora no kujya ku ishuri uri mu mihango. Imbogamizi zihari ni iz'umuco, si uko umubiri wawe utabishoboye.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-008: Hari ibiryo ntagomba kurya cyangwa guteka ndi mu mihango?

Oya. Nta biryo ugomba kwirinda mu gihe cy'imihango, kandi ushobora guteka nk'ibisanzwe. Kurya neza bifasha umubiri wawe.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-009: Nakoresha iki mu gihe cy'imihango? Cotex, igitambaro cyangwa ikindi?

Ushobora gukoresha cotex zikoreshwa rimwe, cotex zongera gukoreshwa, menstrual cup, tampon, amakariso yabugenewe cyangwa igitambaro gisukuye kinyunyuza. Hitamo icyo wisanzuyeho kandi ubona. Gihindure kenshi, uhindurire ahantu hasukuye kandi hiherereye, kandi umese ibyongera gukoreshwa ukoresheje amazi n'isabune.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-010: Nshobora gutwita ndamutse nkoze imibonano mpuzabitsina ndi mu mihango?

Yego, birashoboka, cyane cyane niba ukwezi kwawe ari kugufi cyangwa guhindagurika. Kandi amaraso amwe atari imihango ashobora kwitiranywa nayo. Uburyo bwizewe bwo kwirinda gutwita ni ugukoresha uburyo bwa kijyambere bwo kuboneza urubyaro.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-011: Ubwangavu n'ubugimbi bitangira ryari?

Ku bakobwa, ubwangavu bushobora gutangira ku myaka 8 cyangwa bugatinda kugeza ku myaka 13 cyangwa 14. Ku bahungu, ubugimbi bushobora gutangira ku myaka 9 cyangwa bugatinda kugeza ku myaka 15. Impinduka zose zishobora kumara imyaka myinshi. Buri wese akura ku muvuduko we.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MEN-012: Ni izihe mpinduka ziba ku bakobwa mu bwangavu?

Abakobwa akenshi barakura vuba, amabere akamera, ubwoya bukamera mu maha no ku myanya ndangagitsina. Bamwe bazana ibiheri n'impumuro y'umubiri ikiyongera. Imihango iratangira. Imihango ya mbere bisobanura ko umubiri ushobora gutwita ukoze imibonano idakingiye.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MEN-013: Ni izihe mpinduka ziba ku bahungu mu bugimbi?

Abahungu bamera ubwanwa, ubwoya mu maha no ku myanya ndangagitsina. Barakura, ibitugu bikaguka, ijwi rikaba rinini kandi igitsina kigakura. Bamwe bazana ibiheri cyangwa impumuro y'umubiri. Igitsina gihagarara kenshi, rimwe na rimwe nta mpamvu. Bamwe babyimba gato mu gituza bikazashira.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MEN-014: Nabyutse nsanga natose nijoro. Kurota usohora ni iki kandi ni ibisanzwe?

Kurota usohora ni igihe intanga zisohoka uri mu bitotsi. Ni ibisanzwe mu bugimbi. Kubirota cyangwa kutabirota, byombi ni ibisanzwe.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MEN-015: Kuki amarangamutima yanjye ahinduka cyane muri iyi minsi?

Ubwangavu n'ubugimbi bizana n'impinduka mu marangamutima. Guhindagurika mu byiyumvo, ibyiyumvo bishya by'urukundo n'ubushake bwo kwiherera ni ibisanzwe. Niba wumva ufite agahinda cyangwa impungenge igihe kinini, vugana n'umuntu wizeye cyangwa umukozi w'ubuzima.

*risk: None; sources: PUB; MH*

### MEN-016: Nakurikirana nte imihango yanjye?

Shyira akamenyetso ku munsi wa mbere w'imihango kuri kalendari cyangwa muri telefone. Bara iminsi kuva ku munsi wa mbere w'imihango kugeza ku wa mbere w'ikurikira: ubwo ni ukwezi kwawe k'imihango. Nyuma y'amezi make uzabona uko bigenda, bigufashe kwitegura no kubona impinduka.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-017: Ni ibisanzwe kugira amatembabuzi yera ava mu gitsina?

Yego. Amatembabuzi y'umweru cyangwa asa n'amazi atanuka ni ibisanzwe kandi asukura igitsina akakigira kizima. Ashobora guhinduka mu gihe cy'ukwezi kw'imihango. Jya kwa muganga niba abaye umuhondo, icyatsi cyangwa ikijuju, akanuka nabi, akaryaryata cyangwa akokera.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-018: Imihango yanjye yatinze kandi sindakora imibonano. Kuki?

Imihango ishobora gutinda kubera umunaniro w'ibitekerezo, uburwayi, guhinduka cyane kw'ibiro, imyitozo ikomeye cyangwa ukwezi kukiri gutunganya nyuma y'ubwangavu. Niba utarigeze ukora imibonano, si inda. Niba imihango ihagaze amezi 3 cyangwa arenga, jya kwa muganga.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko ku kigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-019: Ngira impungenge ko igitsina cyanjye ari gito. Ni ibisanzwe?

Ibitsina by'abagabo bigira ingano zitandukanye kandi bikomeza gukura kugeza ubugimbi burangiye, akenshi mu myaka y'ubwangavu ya nyuma. Ingano yacyo kidahagaze ntisobanura ingano yacyo gihagaze, kandi ntibibuza kuba umukunzi mwiza cyangwa kubyara. Niba ubabara cyangwa ufite izindi mpungenge, umukozi w'ubuzima yagusuzuma mu ibanga.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MEN-020: Gusiramura birinda virusi itera SIDA?

Gusiramurwa kwa kiganga bigabanya ibyago umugabo afite byo kwandura virusi itera SIDA ivuye ku mugore ku kigero cya 60%, kandi bikoroshya isuku. Ntibirinda burundu, bityo agakingirizo karacyakenewe. Bikorwa neza kandi ku buntu mu bigo by'ubuzima bya Leta byinshi mu Rwanda.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima cyangwa ibitaro; risk: None; sources: WHO*

### MEN-021: Kwikinisha ni ibisanzwe?

Yego. Kwikinisha bikunze kubaho ku bahungu no ku bakobwa kandi ni uburyo busanzwe bwo kumenya umubiri wawe. Ntibitera ubugumba, intege nke, ubusazi cyangwa ikindi kibazo ku mubiri. Ni amahitamo yawe bwite, kandi no kutabikora nta kibazo.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Kwikoraho ku mubiri wawe ni ibisanzwe mu gukura kandi nta ngaruka mbi bigira. Bikwiye gukorwa mu bwiherero. Niba hari undi muntu ukora ku myanya ndangagitsina yawe cyangwa agusaba gukora ku ye, bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa uhamagare 116.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MEN-022: Nabonye ikibyimba mu ibere. Nkwiye kugira impungenge?

Ibibyimba mu mabere ku bangavu akenshi nta kibazo biba bifite, kandi bikunze guterwa n'imisemburo n'ukwezi kw'imihango. Ariko ikibyimba gishya cyose gikwiye gusuzumwa n'umukozi w'ubuzima, cyane cyane niba gikura, kibabaza cyane, cyangwa hari ibintu bisohoka mu muyoboro w'ibere.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: WHO*

### MEN-023: Imihango yanjye yaje ndi ku ishuri kandi nta cotex mfite. Nakora iki?

Tuza, bibaho ku bakobwa benshi. Saba mwarimu, umuforomo w'ishuri cyangwa inshuti wizeye cotex. Mu gihe utarayibona, ushobora kuzinga impapuro z'isuku cyangwa igitambaro gisukuye ukabishyira mu ikariso. Ziritse umupira mu rukenyerero niba utinya ko amaraso agaragara.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-024: Mbere y'imihango numva mpindagurika mu byiyumvo, inda ikabyimba kandi nkananirwa. Kuki?

Abakobwa benshi bagira ibimenyetso bibanziriza imihango mu minsi mike mbere yayo, nko guhindagurika mu byiyumvo, kubyimba inda, amabere ababara, umunaniro cyangwa ibiheri, biterwa n'impinduka z'imisemburo. Gusinzira, imyitozo, amazi no kurya neza bifasha. Niba ibimenyetso bikabije cyangwa bikubuza ibikorwa bya buri munsi, jya kwa muganga.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-025: Nva amaraso hagati y'imihango ibiri. Ni ikibazo?

Kuva amaraso make hagati y'imihango bishobora kubaho iyo watangiye uburyo bushya bwo kuboneza urubyaro, mu gihe cy'irekurwa ry'igi cyangwa mu myaka ya mbere y'imihango. Ariko kuva amaraso nyuma y'imibonano, amaraso menshi, cyangwa amaraso aherekejwe n'uburibwe cyangwa amatembabuzi anuka bikwiye gusuzumwa n'umukozi w'ubuzima. Niba ushobora kuba utwite, kora ikizamini cyo gutwita.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: MEN*

### MEN-026: Uburibwe bw'imihango ni bwinshi cyane ku buryo imiti igabanya ububabare idafasha.

Uburibwe bukabije bw'imihango bukubuza kujya ku ishuri cyangwa ku kazi, cyangwa budafashwa n'imiti igabanya ububabare, bukwiye gusuzumwa. Bushobora guterwa n'indwara nka endometriose, ivurwa. Jya ku kigo nderabuzima usobanure neza uko uburibwe bukugiraho ingaruka.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: MEN; WHO*

### MEN-027: Nafata iki kugira ngo ngabanye uburibwe bw'imihango?

Gushyira icupa ry'amazi ashyushye ku nda, imyitozo yoroheje n'ikiruhuko akenshi bifasha. Imiti isanzwe igabanya ububabare igurwa muri farumasi, nka paracetamol cyangwa ibuprofen, ishobora kugabanya uburibwe; kurikiza urugero ruri ku gapaki cyangwa ubaze umufarumasiye. Niba uburibwe bukabije, jya kwa muganga.

*Aho wajya: Farumasi cyangwa ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-028: Imihango yanjye ihindagurika cyane kandi mfite ibiheri byinshi n'ubwoya bwinshi ku mubiri.

Imihango ihindagurika cyane hamwe n'ibiheri n'ubwoya bwinshi mu maso cyangwa ku mubiri bishobora kuba ibimenyetso by'ikibazo cy'imisemburo nka PCOS. Birasanzwe kandi bishobora kwitabwaho. Jya kwa muganga agusuzume kandi akugire inama ku buvuzi.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: WHO*

### MEN-029: Umukobwa utarakora imibonano ashobora gukoresha menstrual cup cyangwa tampon?

Yego. Gukoresha menstrual cup cyangwa tampon ntibigira ingaruka ku busugi, kuko ubusugi bujyanye no gukora imibonano, si umubiri. Tangira n'ingano nto, utuze, kandi ukurikize amabwiriza. Niba bikubabaza, hagarara wongere ugerageze nyuma cyangwa ukoreshe cotex.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-030: Najugunya nte cotex nakoresheje?

Pfunyika cotex wakoresheje mu mpapuro cyangwa mu gapfunyika kayo uyishyire mu kimpoteri gipfundikiye cyangwa mu musarane. Ntuyirohe mu musarane w'amazi, kuko ishobora kuwufunga. Karaba intoki nyuma. Cotex zongera gukoreshwa zimeswa n'isabune zikumishirizwa ku zuba.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MEN-031: Numva nzunguye kandi mfite intege nke mu gihe cy'imihango.

Kumva unaniwe gato birasanzwe, ariko kuzungera n'intege nke bishobora kuba ibimenyetso byo kubura amaraso (anemia), cyane cyane niba imihango ari myinshi. Rya ibiryo bikungahaye kuri fer nk'ibishyimbo, imboga z'icyatsi, amagi n'inyama, kandi unywe amazi. Niba uguye igihumure, ufite intege nke cyane cyangwa uva amaraso menshi, jya kwa muganga.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: MEN; WHO*

## Kuboneza urubyaro (Contraception)

### CON-037: Kuboneza urubyaro ni iki?

Kuboneza urubyaro ni uguhitamo niba uzabyara n'igihe uzabyarira, no gukoresha uburyo bwo kwirinda gutwita kugeza igihe witeguye. Uburyo burimo udukingirizo, ibinini, urushinge, agapira ko mu kuboko na IUD. Bumwe bumara umunsi umwe, ubundi amezi cyangwa imyaka, kandi bwose busubirwaho uretse gufunga burundu: ushobora kubyara umaze kubureka. Agakingirizo ni bwo buryo bwonyine burinda na virusi itera SIDA n'indwara zandurira mu mibonano. Umukozi w'ubuzima cyangwa serivisi z'urubyiruko bagufasha guhitamo, mu ibanga.

*risk: None; sources: FP*

### CON-036: Agakingirizo ni iki?

Agakingirizo ni agahu koroheje, akenshi gakozwe muri kawucu (latex), umugabo yambara ku gitsina cye cyafashe umurego mu gihe cy'imibonano. Kabuza intanga z'umugabo kwinjira mu gitsina cy'umugore, bityo kakarinda gutwita, kandi ni bwo buryo bwonyine burinda na virusi itera SIDA n'izindi ndwara zandurira mu mibonano. Koresha agakingirizo gashya buri gihe, kuva imibonano itangiye kugeza irangiye. Hari n'agakingirizo k'abagore umugore yinjiza mu gitsina cye. Udukingirizo tuboneka ku buntu ku bigo nderabuzima no muri serivisi z'urubyiruko, kandi tugurishwa muri farumasi no mu maduka.

*risk: None; sources: FPH*

### CON-038: Ikinini cyo kwirinda gutwita cyihutirwa ni iki?

Ikinini cyo kwirinda gutwita cyihutirwa ni ikinini umugore ashobora gufata nyuma y'imibonano idakingiye, cyangwa iyo agakingirizo kacitse, kugira ngo yirinde gutwita. Gikora neza iyo gifashwe vuba: mu minsi 3 ku binini byinshi, no kugeza ku minsi 5 ku bindi. Si ikinini cyo gukuramo inda: ntigikuraho inda yamaze kujyamo, kandi ntikibuza kubyara nyuma. Ni icy'ibihe byihutirwa, si icyo gukoresha buri gihe. Kibaze ku kigo nderabuzima, muri serivisi z'urubyiruko cyangwa muri farumasi.

*risk: None; sources: EC*

### CON-001: Ni ubuhe buryo buhari bwo kwirinda gutwita?

Hari uburyo bwinshi. Agapira ko mu kuboko (implant) na IUD bikora neza cyane kandi bimara imyaka. Ibinini, inshinge n'ubundi buryo bikora neza iyo bikoreshejwe neza kandi ku gihe. Agakingirizo karinda gutwita kandi kakarinda indwara zandurira mu mibonano. Kubara iminsi no gusohorera hanze ntibyizewe. Umuforomo yagufasha guhitamo ikikubereye.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Inda iterwa gusa n'imibonano. Uburyo bwizewe bwo kuyirinda ku myaka yawe ni ukudakora imibonano. Nukura, hari uburyo butekanye nk'udukingirizo, ibinini n'agapira, kandi umuforomo yabigusobanurira. Niba hari ukuguhatira imibonano, bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa uhamagare 116.

## Gutwita (Pregnancy)

### PRG-027: Gutwita bibaho bite?

Gutwita bitangira iyo intanga ngabo y'umugabo ihuye n'intanga ngore y'umugore. Akenshi bibaho mu mibonano idakingiye: intanga ngabo zinyura muri nyababyeyi zikagera mu muyoboro w'intanga, aho zishobora guhura n'intanga ngore. Intanga ngore yasamye yifata ku rukuta rwa nyababyeyi igakura ikaba umwana mu gihe cy'amezi 9. Umukobwa ashobora gutwita ku mibonano ya mbere, ndetse na mbere y'imihango ye ya mbere, kuko intanga ngore ishobora kurekurwa mbere y'uko imihango ya mbere iza.

*risk: None; sources: PUB; AP*

### PRG-001: Nshobora gutwita bwa mbere nkoze imibonano?

Yego. Iyo umukobwa yatangiye imihango, ashobora gutwita igihe cyose akoze imibonano idakingiye, harimo n'iya mbere.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Yego. Iyo umukobwa yatangiye imihango, imibonano ishobora gutera inda, n'iya mbere. Ku myaka yawe ni byiza gutegereza. Niba hari ukuguhatira cyangwa ukukorakora, ibyo ntibikwiye: bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa uhamagare 116.

*risk: None; sources: PUB; HDI1*

### PRG-002: Namenya nte ko natwise?

Kubura imihango akenshi ni cyo kimenyetso cya mbere. Ibindi bishobora kuba iseseme, amabere ababara no kumva unaniwe cyane. Ikizamini cyo gutwita ku kigo nderabuzima cyangwa kuri farumasi kiguha igisubizo nyacyo.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima cyangwa farumasi; risk: Low; sources: TBC*

### PRG-003: Ndakeka ko natwise kandi mfite ubwoba bwinshi. Sinzi icyo nakora.

Nturi wenyine, kandi ni ibisanzwe kugira ubwoba. Umuforomo kuri serivisi z'urubyiruko yakwemeza niba utwite kandi akakuganiriza mu ibanga ku mahitamo yawe n'intambwe zikurikira. Niba hari uwaguhatiye imibonano, hari ubufasha bw'ubuntu bukugenewe ubu.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko; Ubufasha bwihuse niba wahatiwe; risk: High; sources: AP*

### PRG-004: Gutwita uri ingimbi cyangwa umwangavu ni bibi?

Gutwita ukiri muto bifite ibyago byinshi, nk'umuvuduko w'amaraso ukabije n'indwara ku mubyeyi, n'abana bavuka igihe kitageze cyangwa bafite ibiro bike. Kwipimisha inda hakiri kare ku kigo nderabuzima bigufasha kurinda wowe n'umwana.

**Ku myaka 20-24:** Gutwita mu myaka 20 kuzamura muri rusange bitekanye kurusha mu bwangavu, ariko kwipimisha inda biracyari ngombwa. Tangira kwipimisha mu mezi 3 ya mbere, ufate fer na acide folique, kandi utegure kubyarira ku kigo cy'ubuzima. Guhana intera y'imyaka nibura 2 hagati y'inda birinda wowe n'abana bawe.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima (kwipimisha inda); risk: Low; sources: AP*

### PRG-005: Gukuramo inda biremewe mu Rwanda?

Mu Rwanda, gukuramo inda biremewe iyo uyitwite ari umwana, iyo inda yaturutse ku gufatwa ku ngufu, ku gushyingirwa ku gahato cyangwa ku busambanyi hagati y'abafitanye isano ya bugufi, cyangwa iyo ishyira mu kaga ubuzima bw'umubyeyi cyangwa bw'umwana. Ntibisaba icyemezo cy'urukiko. Kuva iteka rya Minisitiri ryo muri 2024, bishobora gukorerwa ku bigo nderabuzima n'amavuriro byemewe ndetse no mu bitaro, akenshi mbere y'ibyumweru 22. Ku mwana utaruzuza imyaka y'ubukure, ababyeyi ni bo bakunze gusaba, ariko iyo batumvikanye n'umwana, icyifuzo cy'umwana ni cyo cyubahirizwa. Amakuru yawe agomba kubikwa mu ibanga.

*Aho wajya: Muganga wemewe mu kigo cy'ubuvuzi cyemewe; risk: Low; sources: LAW; CRR*

### PRG-006: Inshuti yampaye ibinini byo gukuramo inda. Nabifata nta kibazo?

Ntufate ibinini cyangwa imiti gakondo uhawe n'utari umukozi w'ubuzima. Gukuramo inda mu buryo butizewe ni akaga gakomeye ku buzima bwawe. Banza uvugane na muganga cyangwa umuforomo kugira ngo umenye amahitamo yawe kandi ugume utekanye.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima cyangwa ibitaro; risk: High; sources: AP*

### PRG-007: Umugabo mukuru yamfashe ku ngufu none ndatwite.

Si amakosa yawe, kandi ukwiye kwitabwaho no kurindwa. Isange One Stop Centre itanga ubuvuzi, ubujyanama n'ubufasha mu by'amategeko ku buntu: hamagara 3029. Niba uri munsi y'imyaka 18 ushobora no guhamagara 116. Inda ituruka ku gufatwa ku ngufu ni imwe mu mpamvu zemewe n'amategeko zo kuyikuramo mu Rwanda, kandi muganga yagusobanurira amahitamo yawe.

*Aho wajya: Isange One Stop Centre 3029; 116; risk: Emergency; sources: UNHCR; RNP; LAW*

### PRG-008: Nabyaye vuba. Nabona he ubufasha nk'umubyeyi ukiri muto?

Ibigo nderabuzima bitanga serivisi kuri wowe n'umwana nyuma yo kubyara, harimo no kuboneza urubyaro igihe witeguye. Guhura n'abandi babyeyi bakiri bato nabyo bifasha.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; Itsinda ry'urubyiruko; risk: None; sources: AP*

### PRG-009: Nkwiye gutangira kwipimisha inda ryari?

Tangira ukimara kumenya ko utwite, byiza mu mezi 3 ya mbere, kandi ujye mu bisura byose wahawe. Kwipimisha inda bisuzuma wowe n'umwana, bigapima virusi itera SIDA na mburugu, bigatanga vitamine n'inkingo, kandi bigufasha gutegura kubyarira ku kigo cy'ubuzima mu mutekano.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima (kwipimisha inda); risk: Low; sources: AP; DHS25*

### PRG-010: Ni ibihe bimenyetso by'akaga mu gihe cyo gutwita?

Jya ku kigo cy'ubuzima ako kanya niba uva amaraso mu gitsina, urwaye umutwe cyane cyangwa utabona neza, ubyimba mu maso cyangwa mu ntoki, ufite umuriro, uribwa cyane mu nda, ugagara, amazi akava, cyangwa umwana akareka kunyeganyega. Ibi bishobora kuba bikomeye. Hamagara 112 cyangwa ujye ako kanya ku kigo nderabuzima cyangwa ibitaro bikwegereye.

*Aho wajya: Jya ku kigo cy'ubuzima nonaha; 112; risk: Low; sources: AP; WHO*

### PRG-011: Ndatwite. Nshobora gukomeza amashuri?

Yego. Mu Rwanda, abakobwa batwite n'ababyeyi bakiri bato bashobora gukomeza amasomo no gusubira ku ishuri nyuma yo kubyara. Vugana na mwarimu cyangwa umuyobozi w'ishuri wizeye, kandi ujye kwipimisha inda. Amashuri yawe aracyafite agaciro kandi ejo hazaza hawe haracyari ahawe.

*Aho wajya: Ishuri; serivisi z'urubyiruko; risk: Low; sources: AP*

### PRG-012: Ndakeka ko inda yanjye irimo kuvamo. Nakora iki?

Kuva amaraso no kuribwa mu nda mu ntangiriro yo gutwita bishobora kuba inda ivamo, kandi ukeneye gusuzumwa uyu munsi. Jya ku kigo nderabuzima cyangwa ku bitaro nonaha, cyane cyane niba amaraso ari menshi, ufite umuriro cyangwa wumva ugiye guta ubwenge. Inda kuvamo si amakosa yawe, kandi ubuvuzi n'ubufasha birahari.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima cyangwa ibitaro uyu munsi; risk: High; sources: AP; WHO*

### PRG-013: Nabwira nte ababyeyi banjye ko ntwite?

Ni ibisanzwe kugira ubwoba. Hitamo igihe gituje, cyangwa usabe umuntu mukuru wizeye nka nyogosenge, mwarimu cyangwa umukozi w'ubuzima kukuba hafi cyangwa kugufasha gutangiza ikiganiro. Ababyeyi benshi babanza gutungurwa ariko nyuma bagafasha. Niba utinya guhohoterwa cyangwa kwirukanwa, umukozi w'ubuzima cyangwa Isange (3029) bagufasha kuguma utekanye.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko; risk: Low; sources: AP*

### PRG-014: Umukunzi wanjye aratwite. Nakora iki nk'umubyeyi w'umwana?

Tuza kandi umushyigikire. Jyana nawe ku kigo nderabuzima gupima inda no kwipimisha inda, wumve ibyiyumvo bye, kandi muganire hamwe ku ntambwe zikurikira. Kuba umubyeyi w'umugabo ufite inshingano ni ugushyigikira we n'umwana. Niba ari munsi y'imyaka 18 kandi wowe uri mukuru, ni ikibazo gikomeye mu mategeko.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko; risk: None; sources: AP*

### PRG-015: Nshobora gutwita igitsina kitinjiye neza?

Yego, birashoboka. Amazi asohoka mbere yo gusohora ashobora kuba arimo intanga, kandi intanga ziri hafi y'umunwa w'igitsina zishobora kwinjira. Ibyago ni bike kurusha imibonano yuzuye ariko ntibibura. Koresha agakingirizo cyangwa ubundi buryo, n'ikinini cyihutirwa niba ufite impungenge.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### PRG-016: Imibonano ikoresheje umunwa cyangwa mu kibuno ishobora gutera inda?

Imibonano ikoresheje umunwa cyangwa mu kibuno ntitera inda, keretse intanga zigeze hafi y'igitsina. Ariko ishobora kwanduza indwara zandurira mu mibonano, harimo na virusi itera SIDA, bityo agakingirizo karacyari ngombwa.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### PRG-017: Nkwiye kurya iki igihe ntwite?

Rya ibiryo bitandukanye buri munsi: ibishyimbo n'ibindi bikungahaye kuri poroteyine, imboga, imbuto, ibinyampeke, n'amata cyangwa amagi niba bishoboka. Fata ibinini bya fer na acide folique uhabwa mu kwipimisha inda. Nywa amazi meza, kandi wirinde inzoga n'itabi.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima (kwipimisha inda); risk: None; sources: AP; WHO*

### PRG-018: Nshobora kunywa inzoga cyangwa itabi ntwite?

Oya. Inzoga n'itabi bishobora kwangiza imikurire n'ubwonko bw'umwana kandi bikongera ibyago byo gukuramo inda no kubyara igihe kitageze. Nta rugero rw'inzoga rutagira ingaruka mu gihe cyo gutwita. Niba bikugora kubireka, umukozi w'ubuzima yagufasha.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima (kwipimisha inda); risk: None; sources: AP; WHO*

### PRG-019: Ndumva iseseme kandi ndananiwe mu ntangiriro yo gutwita. Ni ibisanzwe?

Yego. Iseseme, kuruka, umunaniro, amabere ababara no kunyara kenshi birasanzwe mu mezi ya mbere. Rya ibiryo bike kenshi kandi uruhuke. Niba nta biryo cyangwa amazi biguma mu nda, ugatakaza ibiro cyangwa ukumva ufite intege nke cyane, jya ku kigo nderabuzima.

*risk: None; sources: AP*

### PRG-020: Kuki nkwiye kubyarira ku kigo cy'ubuzima?

Abakozi b'ubuzima bashobora gukemura ibibazo bishobora kuvuka bitunguranye mu kubyara, nko kuva amaraso menshi cyangwa umwana ugorwa no guhumeka. Kubyarira ku kigo nderabuzima cyangwa ku bitaro bitekanye kuri wowe n'umwana. Tegura mbere uko uzagerayo igihe ibise bitangiye.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima cyangwa ibitaro; risk: None; sources: AP; DHS25*

### PRG-021: Kuva nabyara numva mfite agahinda kenshi kandi sinshobora kubyihanganira.

Ababyeyi benshi bakimara kubyara bumva bacitse intege iminsi mike, ariko agahinda kamara ibyumweru birenga bibiri, cyangwa gatuma bigorana kwiyitaho cyangwa kwita ku mwana, gashobora kuba agahinda gakabije nyuma yo kubyara. Birasanzwe kandi bivurwa. Vugana n'umukozi w'ubuzima cyangwa uhamagare 114. Niba utekereza kwigirira nabi cyangwa kugirira nabi umwana, shaka ubufasha ako kanya: hamagara 112.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; 114; risk: Low; sources: MH*

### PRG-022: Nkwiye konsa umwana igihe kingana iki?

Ha umwana amashereka gusa mu mezi 6 ya mbere, nta mazi cyangwa ibindi biryo, hanyuma wongereho ibindi biryo ariko ukomeze konsa kugeza ku myaka 2 cyangwa irenga. Amashereka arinda umwana indwara. Umukozi w'ubuzima yagufasha niba konsa bikubabaza cyangwa bikugora.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: WHO*

### PRG-023: Nyuma yo kubyara, nshobora kongera gutwita ryari?

Ushobora kongera gutwita mbere y'uko imihango igaruka, rimwe na rimwe mu byumweru bike nyuma yo kubyara niba utonsa neza gusa. Gutegereza nibura imyaka 2 mbere y'indi nda ni byiza ku buzima bwawe n'ubw'umwana. Baza ibijyanye no kuboneza urubyaro vuba nyuma yo kubyara; uburyo bwinshi ntibugira ingaruka mu gihe wonsa.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: FPH*

### PRG-024: Ikizamini cyo gutwita kigaragaza ko ntatwite ariko imihango ntiraza.

Ikizamini gikozwe kare cyane gishobora kugaragaza ko udatwite n'iyo waba utwite. Tegereza icyumweru wongere wipimishe, ukoresheje inkari za mu gitondo. Niba bikomeza kugaragaza ko udatwite kandi imihango itaraza, umunaniro w'ibitekerezo, guhinduka kw'ibiro cyangwa imisemburo bishobora kuba impamvu; jya kwa muganga niba bikomeje.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: AP*

### PRG-025: Ndatwite kandi mfite uburibwe bukabije ku ruhande rumwe rw'inda.

Uburibwe bukabije ku ruhande rumwe rw'inda yo hasi mu ntangiriro yo gutwita, cyane cyane buherekejwe no kuva amaraso, kuzungera cyangwa kuribwa mu rutugu, bushobora kuba inda yafashe hanze ya nyababyeyi, ikaba ari ikibazo cyihutirwa. Jya ku bitaro bikwegereye ako kanya cyangwa uhamagare 112.

*Aho wajya: Ibitaro nonaha; 112; risk: Emergency; sources: WHO*

### PRG-026: Mfite impungenge ko ntazabyara. Nkwiye kujya kwa muganga ryari?

Urubyiruko rwinshi rushobora kubyara. Niba mumaze amezi 12 mugerageza gutwita mukora imibonano kenshi nta buryo bwo kuboneza urubyaro, mwembi mukwiye kujya kwa muganga. Indwara zandurira mu mibonano zitavuwe ni imwe mu mpamvu zikunze gutera ubugumba, bityo kwipimisha no kwivuza birinda ubushobozi bwawe bwo kubyara.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: WHO*

## Indwara zandurira mu mibonano na SIDA (STIs and HIV)

### HIV-019: Virusi itera SIDA ni iki, na SIDA ni iki?

Virusi itera SIDA ni virusi icogoza ubudahangarwa bw'umubiri, ari bwo burinda umubiri indwara. SIDA ni icyiciro cya nyuma cy'ubwandu bwa virusi itera SIDA, iyo umubiri wacogoye cyane ukarwara indwara zikomeye. Virusi itera SIDA yandurira mu mibonano idakingiye, mu maraso yanduye, no ku mwana akuye kuri nyina; ntiyandurira mu guhoberana, gusangira ibiryo cyangwa ku mibu. Nta muti uyikiza urabaho, ariko imiti ifatwa buri munsi (ARV) ituma abantu bamera neza ikabuza virusi kugera ku cyiciro cya SIDA, kandi iyo virusi itakigaragara mu maraso, ntiyanduza abandi mu mibonano. Kwipimisha ni ubuntu ku bigo nderabuzima.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-001: Indwara zandurira mu mibonano mpuzabitsina ni izihe kandi zandura zite?

Ni indwara zandura cyane cyane binyuze mu mibonano mpuzabitsina, harimo inyuze mu gitsina, mu kibuno n'ikoresheje umunwa. Zimwe zishobora no kwanduza umwana avuye kuri nyina mu gihe atwite, abyara cyangwa yonsa, cyangwa binyuze mu maraso adasuzumwe.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Indwara zandurira mu mibonano ni indwara zandura ziva ku muntu umwe zijya ku wundi cyane cyane mu mibonano. Ntiwazandura uhobera, usangira ibiryo cyangwa umusarane. Kuzimenya ubu bigufasha kwirinda nyuma. Niba ufite ibibazo, baza umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa umukozi w'ubuzima.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-002: Nshobora kurwara indwara yandurira mu mibonano ntabizi?

Yego. Indwara nyinshi zandurira mu mibonano nta bimenyetso zigaragaza. Uburyo bwonyine bwo kumenya ni ukwipimisha.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko ku kigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-003: Imyanya ndangagitsina yanjye irandya kandi hari ibintu binuka bisohoka. Ni indwara yandurira mu mibonano?

Ibi bishobora kuba ibimenyetso by'indwara, ishobora kuba yandurira mu mibonano cyangwa itayandurira. Jya kwa muganga usuzumwe kandi uvurwe. Irinde imibonano cyangwa ukoreshe agakingirizo kugeza ubonanye na muganga.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: STI; HDI1*

### STI-004: Indwara zandurira mu mibonano zirakira?

Nyinshi zirakira. Mburugu, imitezi, chlamydia na trichomonas akenshi zivurwa n'imiti yica mikorobe itanzwe n'umukozi w'ubuzima. Herpes na virusi itera SIDA ntibikira, ariko imiti irabigenzura neza. Hepatite B ivurwa kugira ngo urinde umwijima.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-005: Agakingirizo karinda indwara zose zandurira mu mibonano?

Agakingirizo karinda neza cyane indwara nyinshi, harimo na virusi itera SIDA, iyo gakoreshejwe neza buri gihe. Ntikarinda cyane indwara zitera ibisebe ku ruhu katapfuka, nka herpes cyangwa mburugu.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-006: Hari inkingo z'indwara zandurira mu mibonano?

Yego. Hari inkingo zizewe za HPV, ishobora gutera kanseri y'inkondo y'umura, na hepatite B. Baza ku kigo nderabuzima cyangwa muri gahunda y'ubuzima ku ishuri.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-007: Navuwe indwara yandurira mu mibonano. Umukunzi wanjye nawe akeneye kuvurwa?

Yego. Umukunzi wawe nawe agomba kwipimisha no kuvurwa. Bitabaye ibyo, indwara ishobora kukugarukira cyangwa ikandura abandi.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-008: Bigenda bite iyo indwara yandurira mu mibonano itavuwe?

Indwara zitavuwe zishobora gutera ibibazo bikomeye, nk'indwara zitera ubugumba ku bagore, kanseri zimwe ziterwa na HPV, n'ibyago byinshi byo kwandura virusi itera SIDA. Kwipimisha no kuvurwa hakiri kare birabirinda.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-009: Nshobora kwigurira imiti yica mikorobe nkivura indwara yandurira mu mibonano?

Ni byiza kujya kwa muganga. Indwara zimwe nk'imitezi ntizicyumva imiti myinshi, bityo ukeneye ikizamini nyacyo n'umuti ukwiye.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: STI*

### HIV-001: Virusi itera SIDA yandura ite?

Virusi itera SIDA yandura cyane cyane binyuze mu mibonano mpuzabitsina idakingiye. Ishobora no kuva ku mubyeyi ikajya ku mwana mu gihe atwite, abyara cyangwa yonsa, no binyuze mu maraso adasuzumwe. Agakingirizo karinda neza cyane.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Virusi itera SIDA yandurira mu mibonano idakingiye, mu maraso, no ku mwana akuye kuri nyina. Ntiwayandura ukina, uhobera, usangira ibiryo cyangwa urumwe n'umubu. Abafite virusi itera SIDA babaho igihe kirekire kandi bameze neza iyo bafata imiti.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### HIV-002: Wamenya ko umuntu afite virusi itera SIDA umurebye?

Oya. Abantu benshi bafite virusi itera SIDA basa kandi bumva bameze neza. Kwipimisha ni bwo buryo bwonyine bwo kumenya.

*risk: None; sources: STI; HDI1*

### HIV-003: Hari umuti ukiza virusi itera SIDA?

Ntawo uraboneka. Virusi itera SIDA ntikira, ariko imiti ifatwa buri munsi irayigenzura, kandi abayifite babaho igihe kirekire kandi bameze neza.

*risk: None; sources: STI; HDI1*

### HIV-004: Nakwipimisha virusi itera SIDA he?

Ibigo nderabuzima bitanga ikizamini cya virusi itera SIDA, harimo n'ikizamini cyihuse gitanga igisubizo vuba. Igisubizo cyawe ni ibanga. Serivisi z'urubyiruko ni ahantu heza ho gutangirira.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko ku kigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: STI*

### HIV-005: Ejo nakoze imibonano idakingiye n'umuntu ushobora kuba afite virusi itera SIDA. Nkore iki?

Jya ku kigo nderabuzima uyu munsi. Hari imiti yitwa PEP ishobora kukurinda kwandura virusi itera SIDA nyuma yo guhura n'ibyago. Yitangire vuba ushoboka, byiza mu masaha 24 kandi bitarenze amasaha 72. Ushobora no kubaza ibijyanye no kwirinda gutwita byihutirwa.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye kandi ujye ku kigo nderabuzima uyu munsi: hari umuti witwa PEP urinda virusi itera SIDA iyo utangiwe mu masaha 72. Niba hari uwaguhatiye, si amakosa yawe: jya kuri Isange (3029) cyangwa uhamagare 116.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima uyu munsi; risk: High; sources: PEP; EC*

### HIV-006: PrEP ni iki?

PrEP ni imiti ifatwa n'abantu badafite virusi itera SIDA kugira ngo bagabanye ibyago byo kuyandura. Iboneka nk'ibinini bya buri munsi ndetse n'inshinge. Igenewe abafite ibyago byinshi. Umukozi w'ubuzima yakubwira niba ikubereye.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: PREP*

### HIV-020: PEP ni iki?

PEP ni imiti irinda virusi itera SIDA ifatwa nyuma yo kugira ibyago byo kwandura, urugero nyuma y'imibonano idakingiye, agakingirizo kacitse, cyangwa gufatwa ku ngufu. Igomba gutangirwa vuba bishoboka kandi mu masaha 72 (iminsi 3); uko itangiwe kare ni ko ikora neza. Ifatwa buri munsi mu minsi 28. PEP itangirwa ubuntu ku bigo nderabuzima, ku bitaro no kuri Isange One Stop Centre. Niba utekereza ko wahuye n'ibyago byo kwandura, genda uyu munsi.

*risk: None; sources: PEP*

### HIV-007: Umukunzi wanjye afite virusi itera SIDA kandi afata imiti. Nshobora kuyandura?

Iyo umuntu ufite virusi itera SIDA afata imiti, ingano ya virusi mu maraso ikagera aho itagaragara, ntashobora kuyanduza abo bakorana imibonano. Kwisuzumisha buri gihe birabyemeza. Muganire n'umukozi w'ubuzima uburyo bwo gukomeza kwirinda.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: UEU*

### STI-010: Urukingo rwa HPV ni iki kandi ruhabwa ba nde mu Rwanda?

HPV ni virusi ikunze kuboneka yandurira mu mibonano, ishobora gutera kanseri y'inkondo y'umura nyuma y'igihe. U Rwanda rukingira abakobwa bari mu kigero cy'amashuri HPV, ubu bahabwa doze imwe, kandi muri 2026 rwatangiye gutanga Gardasil 9 irinda ubwoko bwinshi kurushaho. Urukingo rukora neza iyo ruhawe mbere yo gutangira imibonano. Baza ku ishuri cyangwa ku kigo nderabuzima.

*Aho wajya: Gahunda y'ubuzima ku ishuri; ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: HPV26; STI*

### STI-011: Mfite ibisebe bibabaza ku myanya ndangagitsina. Bishobora kuba iki?

Ibisebe bibabaza cyangwa udusebe turimo amazi bishobora guterwa n'indwara nka herpes cyangwa mburugu. Jya kwa muganga vuba usuzumwe kandi uvurwe, kandi wirinde imibonano kugeza icyo gihe. Byinshi mu bitera ibi bivurwa, kandi herpes igenzurwa n'imiti.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: STI*

### STI-012: Ndumva uburyaryate kandi hari amatembabuzi y'umweru afatanye. Ni indwara yandurira mu mibonano?

Uburyaryate n'amatembabuzi y'umweru afatanye akenshi biterwa n'imiyege (candida), ikunze kubaho kandi akenshi itandurira mu mibonano. Ivurwa byoroshye, bityo jya kwa muganga cyangwa ku mufarumasiye. Irinde koza imbere mu gitsina n'amakariso afunze ya pulasitiki. Niba igaruka kenshi cyangwa inuka nabi, isuzumishe izindi ndwara.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima cyangwa farumasi; risk: Low; sources: WHO*

### STI-013: Nokera iyo nyaze kandi nkenera kunyara kenshi. Ni iki?

Ibi akenshi ni indwara y'umuyoboro w'inkari, ikunze kuboneka ku bakobwa n'abagore kandi ikeneye kuvurwa n'umukozi w'ubuzima. Nywa amazi menshi. Niba hari n'amatembabuzi, ibisebe cyangwa uherutse gukora imibonano idakingiye, bishobora kuba indwara yandurira mu mibonano, bityo isuzumishe. Genda vuba niba ufite umuriro cyangwa uribwa mu mugongo.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: WHO*

### STI-014: Hepatite B yandura ite kandi nshobora gukingirwa?

Hepatite B ni virusi yibasira umwijima. Yandurira mu maraso, mu mibonano idakingiye, no ku mwana avuka akuye kuri nyina. Urukingo rwizewe rukurinda ubuzima bwawe bwose, kandi abana bavukira mu Rwanda barakingirwa. Baza ku kigo nderabuzima ibijyanye no kwipimisha no gukingirwa niba utarakingiwe.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: STI; WHO*

### STI-015: Abagore bakwiye gutangira kwisuzumisha kanseri y'inkondo y'umura ryari?

Abagore muri rusange bagirwa inama yo gutangira kwisuzumisha kanseri y'inkondo y'umura kuva ku myaka 30, cyangwa kuva ku myaka 25 ku bafite virusi itera SIDA, bakongera nk'uko umukozi w'ubuzima abagira inama. Kwisuzumisha bibona impinduka hakiri kare, igihe zivurwa byoroshye. Birihuta kandi biboneka mu bigo by'ubuzima byinshi mu Rwanda.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: WHO*

### HIV-008: Nshobora kwipimisha virusi itera SIDA ndi mu rugo?

Yego. Udukoresho two kwipimisha virusi itera SIDA ubwawe tugufasha kwipimisha mu ibanga ukoresheje amacandwe cyangwa igitonyanga cy'amaraso, kandi u Rwanda ruri kutwongera mu bigo by'ubuzima, mu mashuri, muri kaminuza no mu bigo by'urubyiruko. Igisubizo kigaragaza ko wanduye kigomba kwemezwa ku kigo nderabuzima. Igisubizo kigaragaza ko utanduye gishobora kutagaragaza ubwandu bw'amezi 3 ashize.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; farumasi; ibigo by'urubyiruko; risk: None; sources: HIVY*

### HIV-009: Nipimishije nsanga ntanduye nkimara gukora imibonano ifite ibyago. Ndi amahoro?

Ntibiremezwa. Ibizamini bishobora kutabona ubwandu bushya bwa virusi itera SIDA mu byumweru bya mbere, icyo gihe kitwa window period. Ongera wipimishe nyuma y'amezi 3 uhuye n'ibyago. Niba imibonano ifite ibyago yabaye mu masaha 72 ashize, jya nonaha gufata PEP. Koresha agakingirizo kugeza ubyemeje.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: WHO*

### HIV-010: Abantu bafite virusi itera SIDA bashobora gukundana no kubyara?

Yego. Bafata imiti buri munsi, abafite virusi itera SIDA bashobora kugira urukundo ruzima, imibonano n'abana. Iyo virusi itakigaragara mu maraso, ntibayanduza abo bakorana imibonano, kandi bitaweho mu gihe cyo gutwita no konsa, abana bavuka hafi buri gihe nta virusi bafite.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: UEU; WHO*

### HIV-011: Umugore utwite ufite virusi itera SIDA ashobora kurinda umwana?

Yego. Iyo afata imiti mu gihe atwite, abyara n'igihe yonsa, kandi umwana agahabwa imiti imurinda n'ibizamini, ibyago byo kwanduza umwana biba bike cyane. Ni yo mpamvu kwipimisha virusi itera SIDA ari kimwe mu bigize kwipimisha inda.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima (kwipimisha inda); risk: None; sources: WHO*

### HIV-012: Mfite virusi itera SIDA. Nabwira nte umukunzi wanjye?

Kubwira umukunzi ni intambwe yawe bwite kandi ushobora gufata igihe cyo kwitegura. Umujyanama ku kigo nderabuzima yagufasha gutegura icyo uvuga, cyangwa akabimubwirana nawe. Hitamo igihe kiherereye kandi gituje. Niba utinya ihohoterwa, banza uvugane n'umujyanama cyangwa Isange (3029).

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; umujyanama; risk: None; sources: WHO*

### HIV-013: Virusi itera SIDA ingana ite mu rubyiruko mu Rwanda?

Urubyiruko rwinshi mu Rwanda ntirufite virusi itera SIDA, ariko ubwandu bushya mu rubyiruko buracyahangayikishije. Nka 1.2% by'abakobwa na 0.5% by'abahungu bafite imyaka 15 kugeza kuri 24 bafite virusi itera SIDA, kandi urubyiruko rubarirwamo nka kimwe cya gatatu cy'ubwandu bushya. Agakingirizo, kwipimisha, PrEP na PEP bigufasha kwirinda.

*risk: None; sources: HIVY*

### STI-016: Ni ibihe bimenyetso by'imitezi cyangwa chlamydia?

Akenshi nta bimenyetso zigaragaza, cyane cyane ku bakobwa. Iyo bigaragaye, harimo kokera iyo unyara, amatembabuzi adasanzwe ava mu gitsina, uburibwe mu nda yo hasi, cyangwa kuva amaraso nyuma y'imibonano. Zombi zikira iyo zivuwe n'imiti ikwiye itanzwe n'umukozi w'ubuzima, kandi abakunzi nabo bagomba kuvurwa.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: STI*

### STI-017: Mburugu ni iki kandi namenya nte ko nyirwaye?

Mburugu ni indwara yandurira mu mibonano ikunze gutangira ari igisebe kitababaza ku myanya ndangagitsina cyangwa mu kanwa, hanyuma ikaba yatera uduheri ku biganza no ku birenge. Ibimenyetso bishobora kubura ariko indwara igakomeza kwangiza umubiri uko igihe kigenda. Ikizamini cy'amaraso cyoroshye kirayibona, kandi ivurwa n'imiti yica mikorobe.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: STI*

### STI-018: Mfite uduheri duto tumeze nk'udusabe ku myanya ndangagitsina.

Uduheri duto tutababaza dushobora kuba udusabe two ku myanya ndangagitsina, duterwa na HPV. Ntitugira akaga ariko dushobora kwandura binyuze mu gukoranaho kw'uruhu mu mibonano. Umukozi w'ubuzima ashobora kubyemeza akanatuvura. Urukingo rwa HPV rufasha kubirinda.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: STI; HPV26*

### STI-019: Igitsina cyanjye kinuka nk'ifi, cyane cyane nyuma y'imibonano.

Amatembabuzi yoroshye y'ikijuju cyangwa umweru anuka nk'ifi akenshi aterwa n'uguhungabana kw'udukoko dusanzwe mu gitsina, kandi akenshi si indwara yandurira mu mibonano. Ivurwa n'imiti itanzwe n'umukozi w'ubuzima. Irinde koza imbere mu gitsina, kuko bibyongera.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: WHO*

### STI-020: Bigenda bite iyo ngiye kwipimisha indwara zandurira mu mibonano?

Umukozi w'ubuzima azakubaza ibibazo bike mu ibanga, ashobora kureba aho hantu, kandi ashobora gufata inkari, gukora ku gitsina akoresheje agapamba cyangwa gufata amaraso make. Birihuta kandi ni ibanga. Indwara nyinshi zivurwa uwo munsi.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-021: Nshobora kwandura indwara binyuze mu mibonano ikoresheje umunwa?

Yego. Imibonano ikoresheje umunwa ishobora kwanduza indwara nka herpes, imitezi, mburugu na HPV, nubwo ibyago bya virusi itera SIDA ari bike. Gukoresha agakingirizo cyangwa ikindi kintu kibuza gukoranaho bigabanya ibyago.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-022: Nshobora kwandura indwara zandurira mu mibonano ku musarane cyangwa nsangiye igitambaro?

Indwara zandurira mu mibonano zandura cyane cyane binyuze mu mibonano, si ku musarane, muri pisine cyangwa usangiye ibikombe. Mbarwa, nk'inda zo ku myanya ndangagitsina, zishobora kwandura gake binyuze mu bitambaro cyangwa amashuka musangiye, bityo ni isuku nziza kutabisangira.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### STI-023: Imyanya ndangagitsina yanjye irandya cyane nijoro kandi mbona udukoko duto.

Ibi bishobora kuba inda zo ku myanya ndangagitsina cyangwa ibisebe by'uburyaryate (gale), byandura binyuze mu gukoranaho. Bivurwa n'amavuta cyangwa imiti iboneka muri farumasi cyangwa ku kigo nderabuzima. Mesa imyenda n'amashuka mu mazi ashyushye, kandi umukunzi wawe nawe agomba kuvurwa.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima cyangwa farumasi; risk: Low; sources: STI*

### HIV-014: Ni ibihe bimenyetso bya mbere bya virusi itera SIDA?

Abantu benshi nta bimenyetso bagira mu ntangiriro. Bamwe bagira ibimenyetso bisa n'ibicurane nk'umuriro, kuribwa mu muhogo, uduheri cyangwa kubyimba kw'amatakara mu byumweru 2 kugeza kuri 4 nyuma yo kwandura, hanyuma bigashira. Ntushobora kubimenya ukurikije ibimenyetso; uburyo bwonyine ni ukwipimisha.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: WHO*

### HIV-015: Nshobora kwandura virusi itera SIDA binyuze mu gusomana, guhoberana cyangwa gusangira ibiryo?

Oya. Virusi itera SIDA ntiyandurira mu gusomana, guhoberana, gusuhuzanya, gusangira ibiryo, ibikombe cyangwa umusarane, cyangwa kurumwa n'umubu. Yandurira mu maraso, mu mibonano idakingiye, no ku mwana akuye kuri nyina.

*risk: None; sources: WHO*

### HIV-016: Ndi urubyiruko rufite virusi itera SIDA kandi ndambiwe gufata ibinini buri munsi.

Urubyiruko rwinshi rwumva rutyo, kandi ibyiyumvo byawe birumvikana. Gufata imiti buri munsi bikurinda kurwara kandi bituma virusi itagaragara mu maraso bityo ntuyanduze abandi. Vugana n'umukozi w'ubuzima ku ngaruka z'imiti, uburyo bwo kwibutswa, cyangwa kwinjira mu itsinda ry'urubyiruko rufite virusi itera SIDA.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: WHO; UEU*

### HIV-017: PEP ifatwa igihe kingana iki kandi igira ingaruka?

PEP ifatwa buri munsi iminsi 28, nta kwibagirwa. Bamwe bumva bananiwe, iseseme cyangwa ibibazo by'igifu mu ntangiriro; akenshi bigenda bigabanuka. Ntuhagarike mbere y'igihe. Subira kwipimisha virusi itera SIDA nyuma yo kurangiza nk'uko wabigiriwemo inama.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: PEP*

### HIV-018: Nshobora kwandura virusi itera SIDA binyuze mu rwembe, tatuwaje cyangwa gutobora amatwi?

Yego, niba ibikoresho biriho amaraso y'undi muntu. Ntugasangire urwembe cyangwa inshinge, kandi ukoreshe tatuwaje cyangwa utobore amatwi gusa ahakoreshwa inshinge nshya kandi zisukuye.

*risk: None; sources: WHO*

## Urukundo no kwemera (Consent and relationships)

### REL-001: Kwemera (consent) bisobanura iki?

Kwemera bisobanura kuvuga yego ku bushake kandi bigaragara kuri buri gikorwa cy'imibonano. Umuntu wese ashobora guhindura ibitekerezo igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Guceceka, guhatirwa, iterabwoba, ubwoba cyangwa gusinda si yego.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Kwemera bisobanura ko umubiri wawe ari uwawe. Nta muntu, n'iyo yaba uwo uzi cyangwa ukunda, ukwiye kugukoraho mu buryo udashaka. Buri gihe ushobora kuvuga oya, kandi niba hari ukukoraho nabi cyangwa agusaba kubigira ibanga, bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa uhamagare 116.

*risk: None; sources: TBC; HDI2*

### REL-002: Umukunzi wanjye avuga ko niba mukunda nzaryamana nawe.

Ntugomba kwerekana urukundo ukoresheje imibonano. Ugukunda azubaha icyemezo cyawe ategereze kugeza witeguye, cyangwa abyemere niba utabishaka na rimwe. Guhatirwa si urukundo.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Urukundo ntirugaragazwa n'imibonano, kandi ku myaka yawe ni byiza gutegereza. Niba hari ukuguhatira, cyane cyane umuntu mukuru, bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa uhamagare 116.

*risk: Low; sources: HDI2*

### REL-003: Avuga ko azantana niba ntaryamanye nawe.

Ibyo ni uguhatirwa, kandi si byiza. Umubiri wawe ni amahitamo yawe. Umukunzi mwiza ntakoresha iterabwoba. Niba wumva udatekanye cyangwa uhatirwa, ubufasha burahari.

*Aho wajya: Ubufasha bwihuse niba wahatiwe; risk: Low; sources: HDI2*

### REL-004: Inshuti zanjye zirampa urw'amenyo kuko ntarakora imibonano.

Urubyiruko rwinshi rurategereza, kandi ni icyemezo cyiza. Ni wowe uhitamo igihe witeguye, si inshuti zawe. Inshuti nyazo zubaha amahitamo yawe.

*risk: None; sources: HDI2*

### REL-005: Umugabo mukuru ampa amafaranga n'impano none arashaka ko turyamana.

Impano cyangwa amafaranga ntibisobanura ko hari uwo ugomba imibonano. Imibanire n'abagabo bakuru batanga amafaranga ikunze kuzana igitutu n'ibyago. Niba uri munsi y'imyaka 18, kuryamana n'umuntu mukuru ni icyaha mu Rwanda. Ushobora kubona ubufasha mu ibanga: hamagara 116 cyangwa 3029.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Umuntu mukuru uguha amafaranga cyangwa impano ashaka ko muryamana aba akora ikintu kibi cyane kandi kinyuranyije n'amategeko. Si amakosa yawe. Ntubigire ibanga: bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa uhamagare umurongo w'ubuntu wo gufasha abana 116.

*Aho wajya: 116; Isange 3029; risk: High; sources: HDI2; LAW; RNP; UNHCR*

### REL-006: Ni bibi kubaza umukobwa niba ashaka ko dukora imibonano?

Kubaza ni byo bikwiye. Kubaza no kubaha igisubizo, cyaba yego cyangwa oya, ni byo kwemera. Ntukomeze guhatiriza nyuma yo kubwirwa oya.

*risk: None; sources: HDI2*

### REL-007: Umukunzi wanjye asaka telefone yanjye kandi akambwira abo nemerewe kuvugisha.

Kugenzura abo ubona cyangwa abo uvugisha ni uburyo bw'ihohoterwa mu rukundo. Ukwiye kwizerwa no kugira ubwisanzure. Vugana n'umuntu wizeye, kandi niba wumva udatekanye, ubufasha burahari.

*Aho wajya: Umurongo wo kurwanya ihohoterwa 3512; Isange 3029; risk: High; sources: VAW*

### REL-008: Umukunzi wanjye arankubita iyo dutonganye. Ese gutongana si ibisanzwe mu bakundana?

Kutumvikana birabaho, ariko gukubita si ibisanzwe kandi si byiza na gato. Ni ihohoterwa. Ushobora kubona ubufasha mu ibanga: Isange One Stop Centre 3029 cyangwa umurongo wo kurwanya ihohoterwa 3512. Niba uri mu kaga ubu, hamagara 112.

*Aho wajya: Isange One Stop Centre 3029 (ni ubuntu); umurongo wo gufasha abana 116; Umurongo wo kurwanya ihohoterwa 3512; ibyihutirwa 112; risk: High; sources: VAW; HDI2; RIB*

### REL-009: Naganiriza nte ababyeyi banjye ku mibonano n'urukundo?

Biragora urubyiruko rwinshi, ndetse n'ababyeyi benshi. Gerageza igihe gituje kandi kiherereye, utangire n'ikibazo rusange, cyangwa ubanze uganirize undi muntu mukuru wizeye nka nyogosenge cyangwa nyokorome. Ushobora no gukoresha Intyoza ubanza kumenya amakuru.

*risk: None; sources: HDI1; HDI2*

### REL-010: Ni ku yihe myaka kuryamana byemewe n'amategeko mu Rwanda?

Amategeko y'u Rwanda ahana umuntu mukuru usambanya umwana igifungo cy'imyaka 20 kugeza kuri 25, n'igifungo cya burundu iyo umwana ari munsi y'imyaka 14. Imibonano hagati y'abana bafite imyaka 14 kuzamura nta gahato cyangwa iterabwoba ntihanwa. Imyaka yemewe yo gushyingirwa ni 21.

**Ku myaka 20-24:** Imibonano hagati y'abantu bakuru babyemeranyijwe iremewe. Ariko umuntu mukuru ukora imibonano n'umuntu uri munsi y'imyaka 18 aba asambanyije umwana, bihanishwa igifungo cy'imyaka 20 kugeza kuri 25, cyangwa igifungo cya burundu iyo umwana ari munsi y'imyaka 14. Imyaka yemewe yo gushyingirwa ni 21.

*risk: None; sources: LAW; HDI2*

### REL-011: Namenya nte ko urukundo rwanjye ari rwiza?

Urukundo rwiza rurangwa no kubahana, kwizerana no kuvugisha ukuri. Mwembi mushobora kuvuga oya, gufatira ibyemezo hamwe no kumarana igihe n'inshuti n'umuryango. Ubwoba, iterabwoba cyangwa kugenzurwa ni ibimenyetso byo kwitondera.

*risk: None; sources: HDI2; VAW*

### REL-012: Nakwanga imibonano nte ntatakaje umukunzi wanjye?

Ushobora kuvuga neza kandi bigaragara: 'Ndagukunda, ariko sinditegura imibonano.' Ntugomba gusobanurira umuntu impamvu. Tanga ibindi bitekerezo byo kumarana igihe. Umukunzi ukwitayeho by'ukuri azubaha oya yawe; niba agiye kubera yo, bikwereka uwo ari we, si agaciro kawe.

*risk: None; sources: HDI2*

### REL-013: Namenya nte ko niteguye gukora imibonano?

Kwitegura bisobanura ko ubishaka ku bwawe, atari ukubera igitutu, wizeye kandi ushobora kuganira neza n'umukunzi wawe, mwembi mubyemeranyijeho, kandi uzi uko mwakwirinda inda n'indwara. Niba ufite gushidikanya, ubwoba cyangwa uhatirwa, ntiwiteguye, kandi gutegereza ni byiza buri gihe.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Ku myaka yawe, umubiri wawe n'ibyiyumvo byawe biracyakura, kandi ni byiza gutegereza. Gukunda umuntu cyangwa kumwiyumvamo ni ibisanzwe. Niba hari umuntu, cyane cyane umuntu mukuru, ushaka ko muryamana, ibyo ntibikwiye: bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa uhamagare 116.

*risk: None; sources: HDI2*

### REL-014: Ni byiza koherereza umukunzi wanjye amafoto nambaye ubusa?

Bifite ibyago. Iyo umaze kohereza ifoto, ntuba ukiyigenzura: ishobora gusakazwa, kubikwa nyuma yo gutandukana cyangwa gukoreshwa mu kukukangisha. Gusakaza amashusho y'urukozasoni y'umuntu uri munsi y'imyaka 18 ni icyaha. Ushobora buri gihe kwanga. Niba hari ukangisha gusakaza amafoto yawe, si amakosa yawe: bika ubutumwa kandi ubimenyeshe RIB kuri 166 cyangwa umurongo wo kurwanya ihohoterwa 3512.

*Aho wajya: RIB 166 niba bagukangisha; risk: Low; sources: RIB*

### REL-015: Kureba filime z'urukozasoni ni bibi kuri njye?

Filime z'urukozasoni si ubuzima nyabwo: akenshi zerekana imibonano nta kwemeranya cyangwa kwirinda, n'imibiri idasanzwe. Kuzireba cyane bishobora gutera igitutu n'ibitekerezo bitari byo ku mibonano n'abakunzi. Niba bigutera kumva nabi cyangwa udashobora kubireka, vugana n'umuntu wizeye cyangwa umujyanama.

**Ku myaka 10-14:** Filime z'urukozasoni zakorewe abantu bakuru kandi ntizerekana imibanire nyayo irimo kubahana. Niba hari ukwereka amafoto cyangwa amashusho y'urukozasoni, ibyo ntibikwiye: bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa uhamagare 116. Niba warabonye ikintu cyakubabaje, kukivugaho bifasha.

*risk: None; sources: HDI2*

### REL-016: Twatandukanye kandi numva meze nabi cyane. Nakwihangana nte?

Gutandukana birababaza, kandi ni ibisanzwe kumva agahinda, uburakari cyangwa urujijo. Ganira n'inshuti cyangwa umuryango, komeza ukore ibyo ukunda, usinzire kandi urye neza, kandi wihe igihe. Irinde guhora ureba imbuga nkoranyambaga ze. Niba agahinda kamara ibyumweru cyangwa utekereza kwigirira nabi, shaka ubufasha: hamagara 114 cyangwa uvugane n'umukozi w'ubuzima.

*risk: None; sources: MH*

### REL-017: Ni byiza guhura n'umuntu twamenyaniye kuri interineti?

Witonde: abantu bo kuri interineti si ko buri gihe baba aribo bavuga. Ntutange aho utuye, ishuri wigaho cyangwa amafoto yawe bwite. Niba wahisemo guhura nawe, muhurire ahantu hahurira abantu benshi ku manywa, ubwire umuntu wizeye aho ugiye, kandi ugende niba wumva utisanzuye. Witondere cyane umuntu wese uguha amafaranga, impano cyangwa akazi.

*risk: None; sources: SAFE*

### REL-018: Nabwira nte umuntu ko mukunda?

Ba uwo uri we kandi bivuge mu buryo bworoshye, urugero: 'Nkunda kumarana igihe nawe. Wakwemera ko twasohokana?' Hitamo igihe kiherereye kandi gituje. Itegure igisubizo icyo ari cyo cyose: niba avuze oya, ubyubahe, kubaza bisaba ubutwari kandi ntibisobanura ko hari ikibi kuri wowe.

*risk: None; sources: HDI2*

### REL-019: Nkururwa n'abantu bahuje igitsina nanjye. Hari ikibazo mfite?

Nta kibazo ufite. Urubyiruko rwinshi rugira ibibazo ku byiyumvo byo gukururwa n'abandi, kandi ibyiyumvo bishobora kugenda bisobanuka uko igihe kigenda. Ukwiye kubahwa, umutekano n'ubuvuzi bwiza nk'abandi bose. Kuganira n'umuntu wizeye cyangwa umujyanama byagufasha, kandi serivisi z'ubuzima, harimo kwirinda virusi itera SIDA, ni iz'abantu bose.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko; umujyanama; risk: None; sources: MH*

### REL-020: Ni byiza gutegereza kugeza nshatse?

Yego. Guhitamo gutegereza ni amahitamo meza kandi y'ubuzima, kandi ni bwo buryo bwizewe bwo kwirinda inda n'indwara. Ariko ni byiza no kumenya ibijyanye no kwemera, kuboneza urubyaro n'indwara, kugira ngo uzaba witeguye kwirinda igihe cyose uzabihitamo.

*risk: None; sources: HDI2*

### REL-021: Inshuti zanjye zimpatira kunywa inzoga mu birori. Nakora iki?

Ushobora kwanga: 'Oya urakoze, meze neza.' Inzoga zituma bigorana gufata ibyemezo bitekanye ku mibonano no kwemera, kandi zongera ibyago by'ihohoterwa. Guma hamwe n'inshuti wizeye, uhore ufite icyo kunywa cyawe, kandi utegure uko utaha mu mutekano.

*risk: Low; sources: HDI2; MH*

### REL-022: Umuntu twamenyaniye kuri interineti ansaba amafaranga cyangwa amafoto yanjye bwite.

Witonde: ubu ni uburiganya bukunze kubaho kandi bushobora gutuma bagukangisha. Ntukohereze amafaranga cyangwa amafoto, reka kumuvugisha kandi umuhagarike (block). Niba agukangisha, bika ubutumwa kandi ubimenyeshe RIB kuri 166. Si amakosa yawe.

*Aho wajya: RIB 166; risk: Low; sources: RIB*

### REL-023: Narinda nte konti zanjye z'imbuga nkoranyambaga?

Koresha amagambo y'ibanga akomeye kandi ntuyasangize, n'umukunzi wawe. Fungura uburyo bwo kwemeza mu ntambwe ebyiri, shyira konti zawe mu ibanga, kandi ntushyireho aho utuye, ishuri cyangwa aho uri. Tekereza mbere yo gushyiraho: iyo ifoto imaze gusakazwa, ishobora gukwirakwira.

*risk: None; sources: RIB*

### REL-024: Abantu barantoteza kuri interineti.

Gutotezwa kuri interineti si amakosa yawe. Ntubasubize, fata amafoto y'ubutumwa (screenshots), uhagarike kandi urege konti zabo ku rubuga. Bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye. Niba hari iterabwoba cyangwa amafoto y'urukozasoni, bimenyeshe RIB kuri 166, cyangwa 116 niba uri munsi y'imyaka 18. Vugana n'umuntu niba bigira ingaruka ku byiyumvo byawe.

*Aho wajya: RIB 166; 116; risk: Low; sources: RIB; MH*

### REL-025: Umuntu yasakaje ifoto y'umunyeshuri twigana yambaye ubusa mu itsinda ryacu.

Ntuyisakaze kandi uyisibe. Gusakaza amashusho y'urukozasoni y'umuntu atabyemeye, cyane cyane uri munsi y'imyaka 18, ni icyaha kandi bimubabaza cyane. Bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye cyangwa ubimenyeshe RIB kuri 166. Shyigikira mugenzi wawe aho kwifatanya n'abandi.

*Aho wajya: RIB 166; risk: Low; sources: RIB; LAW*

### REL-026: Jye n'umukunzi wanjye dutongana kenshi. Twavugana neza dute?

Hitamo igihe gituje, muvuge uko mwiyumva mukoresheje amagambo atangirwa na 'Njye', mutegane amatwi nta guca undi mu ijambo, kandi mushakire hamwe igisubizo. Mufate akaruhuko niba ibintu bishyushye. Gutongana birabaho, ariko ibitutsi, iterabwoba cyangwa ihohoterwa ntibikwiye na rimwe.

*risk: None; sources: HDI2*

### REL-027: Ndakeka ko umukunzi wanjye amca inyuma.

*Aho wajya: 114; ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: HL; MH*

### EMO-014: Sinsinzira nijoro kubera gutekereza cyane.

Gerageza gahunda: uryame kandi ubyuke ku masaha amwe, ushyire telefone kure isaha imwe mbere yo kuryama, wirinde ikawa n'ibinyobwa byongera imbaraga nijoro, kandi wandike impungenge zawe mbere yo kuryama. Niba kudasinzira bimara ibyumweru cyangwa bikazana agahinda, vugana n'umukozi w'ubuzima.

*risk: None; sources: MH*

### EMO-015: Maze iminsi ntameze neza.

Mbabajwe no kumva ko utameze neza. Byamfasha kumenya byinshi: ni umubiri, nk'uburibwe, umuriro, kuva amaraso cyangwa amatembabuzi, cyangwa ni amarangamutima, nk'agahinda, stress cyangwa impungenge? Niba ufite umuriro, uburibwe bukabije cyangwa uva amaraso, jya ku kigo nderabuzima. Ushobora no kuvugana n'umukozi w'ubuzima cyangwa guhamagara umurongo w'ubuntu 114.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; 114; risk: Low; sources: MH*

### EMO-016: Sinshaka guhamagara umuntu cyangwa gushaka ubufasha.

Ndakumva, kandi nta kibazo niba udashaka guhamagara ubu. Ndacyari hano. Ese hari umuntu wizeye uri hafi yawe, inshuti cyangwa uwo mu muryango, wabwira ko utameze neza ukamusaba kuguma nawe? Kwandika uko wiyumva nabyo bishobora gufasha. Niba wumva ushobora kwigirira nabi ubu, hamagara 112 cyangwa ujye ku kigo nderabuzima kikwegereye.

*Aho wajya: 114; 112; risk: High; sources: MH*

### EMO-017: Numva nshaka kwiyangiza. Nakora iki ubu?

Urakoze kwandika. Ubu, jya ahantu hatekanye hari abandi bantu, shyira kure icyo cyose wakwangiza nacyo, kandi uvugane n'umuntu wizeye cyangwa uhamagare umurongo w'ubuntu 114. Humeka buhoro kandi wibuke ko ubwo bushake buzashira. Niba uri mu kaga, hamagara 112.

*Aho wajya: 114; 112; risk: High; sources: MH*

### EMO-018: Papa wanjye yarapfuye kandi sinshobora kureka kubabara.

Mbabarira cyane. Agahinda ko gupfusha gashobora kuzana umubabaro, uburakari, kwicira urubanza n'umunaniro, kandi kaza kagenda. Emera kubyumva, uvuge kuri uwo muntu n'abantu wizeye, kandi ukomeze gahunda ya buri munsi. Niba agahinda kadahosha uko igihe kigenda cyangwa kakakubuza kubaho, vugana n'umujyanama cyangwa uhamagare 114.

*Aho wajya: 114; ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: MH*

### EMO-019: Ndakara vuba cyane kandi rimwe na rimwe nkubita ibintu.

Uburakari ni ibyiyumvo bisanzwe, ariko kubukurikiza bishobora kukubabaza no kubabaza abandi. Iyo wumva buzamuka, va aho uri, uhumeke buhoro ubare kugeza ku icumi, cyangwa ugende gato. Imyitozo no kuvuga icyakurakaje bifasha. Niba uburakari bukunze kukurenga, vugana n'umujyanama.

*risk: Low; sources: MH*

### EMO-020: Umuryango wanjye unyitezeho byinshi cyane kandi mpora numva igitutu.

Ibyo umuryango ukwitezeho bishobora kuremera. Gerageza kubwira umubyeyi cyangwa uwo mu muryango wizeye uko wiyumva mu kuri, kandi wibande ku gukora uko ushoboye aho gushaka kuba intagereranywa. Fata igihe cyo kuruhuka no gukora ibyo ukunda. Niba igitutu kigutera guhangayika cyane cyangwa agahinda, vugana n'umujyanama.

*risk: Low; sources: MH*

### EMO-021: Imbuga nkoranyambaga zituma numva meze nabi kuri njye.

Ibyinshi bishyirwa ku mbuga ni ibihe byahinduwe kandi byatunganyijwe, si ubuzima nyabwo. Niba wumva umerewe nabi nyuma yo kureba, gerageza guhagarika konti zigutera kwigereranya, kwishyiriraho igihe ntarengwa, no kumara igihe kinini mu bikorwa bitari kuri interineti no hamwe n'abantu bagushyigikira.

*risk: None; sources: MH*

### EMO-022: Numva umubiri wanjye udahuye n'uwo ndi we imbere.

Urakoze gusangiza ikintu cyawe bwite gutya. Ibibazo ku bijyanye n'igitsina umuntu yiyumvamo bibaho ku rubyiruko rumwe, kandi ibyiyumvo byawe bikwiye kubahwa. Kuganira n'umujyanama cyangwa umukozi w'ubuzima wizeye byagufasha kubisobanukirwa mu mutekano. Ukwiye umutekano n'ubuvuzi bwiza.

*Aho wajya: Umujyanama; serivisi z'urubyiruko; risk: None; sources: MH*

### EMO-023: Nahagaritse kurya kuko nshaka kunanuka.

Urakoze kubimbwira. Kutarya bishobora kwangiza cyane umubiri n'ubwenge, kandi ibi byiyumvo biragoye kubyihanganira wenyine. Vugana n'umukozi w'ubuzima vuba, cyangwa uhamagare umurongo w'ubuntu 114. Kubwira umuntu wizeye nabyo byagufasha. Ukwiye gufashwa.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; 114; risk: High; sources: MH*

### EMO-024: Inshuti yanjye isa n'ifite agahinda igihe cyose. Nayifasha nte?

Mubaze buhoro uko ameze kandi umutege amatwi utamucira urubanza. Mumenyeshe ko umwitayeho kandi mumarane igihe. Mushishikarize kuganira n'umujyanama cyangwa guhamagara 114, kandi wiyemeze kumuherekeza. Niba avuga ku kwiyahura cyangwa kwiyangiza, bibwire umuntu mukuru wizeye ako kanya.

*risk: None; sources: MH*

## Ibihuha (Myths and misconceptions)

### MYT-001: Gukora imibonano bikiza ibiheri?

Si byo. Ibiheri bikunze kuza mu bwangavu n'ubugimbi kubera imisemburo. Imibonano ntibikiza. Umukozi w'ubuzima yagufasha niba ari byinshi cyane.

*risk: None; sources: PUB; HDI1; HDI2*

### MYT-002: Imibonano ituma amabere cyangwa ikibuno bikura?

Si byo. Amabere n'imiterere y'umubiri bihinduka kubera imisemburo y'ubwangavu n'uruhererekane, si imibonano.

*risk: None; sources: PUB; HDI1*

### MYT-003: Gukora imibonano bihagarika kuribwa mu mihango?

Si byo. Imibonano si umuti wo kuribwa mu mihango. Niba ububabare bukubuza ibikorwa bya buri munsi, jya kwa muganga.

*risk: None; sources: MEN; HDI1*

### MYT-004: Umukobwa akura vuba nyuma yo gukora imibonano bwa mbere?

Si byo. Gukura biterwa n'ubwangavu kandi bifite igihe cyabyo. Imibonano ntibihindura.

*risk: None; sources: PUB; HDI1*

### MYT-005: Ntushobora gutwita ku nshuro ya mbere.

Si byo. Gutwita bishobora kubaho igihe cyose habayeho imibonano idakingiye, harimo n'iya mbere.

*risk: None; sources: PUB; HDI1*

### MYT-006: Kuboneza urubyaro bituma umuntu atabyara.

Si byo. Uburyo bwa kijyambere ntibutera ubugumba.

*risk: None; sources: FP; HDI2*

### MYT-007: Udukingirizo dufite utwobo duto virusi itera SIDA inyuramo.

Si byo. Udukingirizo ntidufite utwobo virusi itera SIDA ishobora kunyuramo, kandi ntiturimo virusi itera SIDA.

*risk: None; sources: FPHM*

### MYT-008: Udukingirizo dutuma abagabo bacika intege cyangwa ntibabyare.

Si byo. Udukingirizo ntidutera abagabo ubugumba, intege nke cyangwa kudahagarika, kandi ntitugabanya ubushake bw'imibonano.

*risk: None; sources: FPHM*

### MYT-009: Agakingirizo gashobora kubura mu mubiri w'umugore.

Si byo. Agakingirizo ntigashobora kubura mu mubiri w'umugore. Niba kavuyemo imbere, gashobora gukurwamo, kandi uburyo bwo kwirinda gutwita byihutirwa bushobora gufasha.

*risk: None; sources: FPHM; FPH*

### MYT-010: Udukingirizo ni utw'indaya cyangwa abaca inyuma abo bashakanye.

Si byo. Abashakanye n'abandi benshi bakoresha udukingirizo. Kugakoresha bigaragaza ko witaye ku buzima bwawe.

*risk: None; sources: FPHM; HDI1*

### MYT-011: Udukingirizo dukuraho uburyohe bwose.

Ni ukuri ku bantu bamwe. Abagabo bamwe bumva uburyohe bugabanutse, ariko igitonyanga kimwe cyangwa bibiri by'amavuta ashingiye ku mazi imbere mu gakingirizo bifasha. Kuganira n'umukunzi wawe nabyo bifasha.

*risk: None; sources: FPH; HDI1*

### MYT-012: Virusi itera SIDA irakira.

Si byo. Virusi itera SIDA ntikira, ariko imiti irayigenzura bigatuma abantu babaho igihe kirekire kandi bameze neza.

*risk: None; sources: STI; HDI1*

### MYT-013: Kwisukura nyuma y'imibonano birinda gutwita n'indwara.

Si byo. Kwisukura cyangwa koza imbere ntibirinda gutwita cyangwa indwara zandurira mu mibonano, kandi koza imbere byongera ibyago byo kwandura.

*risk: None; sources: FPH*

### MYT-014: Koga uri mu mihango bitera ubugumba.

Si byo. Kwisukura uri mu mihango ni byiza kandi ntibitera ubugumba.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MYT-015: Ukuyemo inda urapfa cyangwa ugafungwa.

Si ko bimeze. Gukuramo inda bikozwe n'abatabizobereyemo ni akaga gakomeye. Mu Rwanda, gukuramo inda bikozwe na muganga wemewe biremewe ku mpamvu zimwe, nk'iyo uyitwite ari umwana cyangwa nyuma yo gufatwa ku ngufu.

*Aho wajya: Muganga wemewe mu bitaro byemewe; risk: Low; sources: AP; LAW; HDI2*

### MYT-016: Akarangabusugi kerekana niba umukobwa ari isugi.

Si byo. Akarangabusugi ni agahu koroshye kandi kagurumbuka, gatandukana ku mukobwa ku wundi. Gashobora kugurumbuka kubera siporo cyangwa gukoresha tampon, kandi abakobwa benshi ntibava amaraso ku nshuro ya mbere bakora imibonano. Nta kizamini gishobora kwemeza ubusugi, kandi ibyitwa ibizamini by'ubusugi bigira ingaruka mbi.

*risk: None; sources: WHO*

### MYT-017: Kwikinisha bitera ubugumba cyangwa ubusazi.

Si byo. Kwikinisha ntibitera ubugumba, ubusazi, intege nke, ubuhumyi cyangwa igitsina gito. Ni imyitwarire isanzwe.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MYT-018: Amaraso y'imihango ni umwanda cyangwa ashobora gukoreshwa mu burozi.

Si byo. Amaraso y'imihango ni amaraso asanzwe n'uturemangingo biva muri nyababyeyi. Si umwanda, si akaga kandi nta bupfumu afite. Abakobwa ntibakwiye na rimwe kugira isoni z'imihango.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MYT-019: Kurya ibiryo runaka cyangwa kunywa ikintu birinda gutwita.

Si byo. Nta biryo, ikinyobwa, imiti gakondo cyangwa soda birinda gutwita. Uburyo bwa kijyambere nk'udukingirizo, ibinini, inshinge, agapira, IUD n'ibinini byihutirwa ni bwo bukora.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MYT-020: Abafite abakunzi benshi ni bo bonyine barwara indwara zandurira mu mibonano.

Si byo. Umuntu wese ukora imibonano ashobora kwandura indwara, n'iyo yaba afite umukunzi umwe, kuko indwara nyinshi nta bimenyetso zigaragaza. Udukingirizo no kwipimisha birinda buri wese.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### MYT-021: Abagabo basiramuye ntibakeneye udukingirizo.

Si byo. Gusiramurwa bigabanya ibyago bya virusi itera SIDA ariko ntibiyirinda burundu, kandi ntibirinda gutwita cyangwa izindi ndwara nyinshi. Udukingirizo turacyakenewe.

*risk: None; sources: WHO*

### MYT-022: Imibonano mu gihe cyo gutwita igira ingaruka ku mwana.

Akenshi si byo. Mu gutwita gusanzwe, imibonano ntigira ingaruka ku mwana, urinzwe imbere muri nyababyeyi. Umukozi w'ubuzima ashobora kugira inama yo kwirinda imibonano niba hari kuva amaraso, amazi ava cyangwa ibindi bibazo. Udukingirizo turacyarinda indwara.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima (kwipimisha inda); risk: None; sources: AP*

### MYT-023: Agapira ko mu kuboko gashobora kugenda mu mubiri cyangwa kakabura.

Si byo. Agapira kaguma aho kashyizwe munsi y'uruhu rw'ukuboko. Ni gake cyane kigira hirya gato, kandi umukozi w'ubuzima ashobora kukabona akakakuramo.

*risk: None; sources: FPH*

### MYT-024: Uburyo bwo kuboneza urubyaro butera ubumuga ku mwana cyangwa kanseri.

Si byo. Uburyo bwa kijyambere bwo kuboneza urubyaro ntibutera ubumuga ku bana, kandi ntibutera kanseri; bumwe ndetse bugabanya ibyago bya kanseri zimwe. Ni imwe mu miti yakorewe ubushakashatsi bwinshi ku isi.

*risk: None; sources: FP*

### MYT-025: Abahungu barwara iyo badakora imibonano.

Si byo. Kudakora imibonano ntibigira ingaruka ku bahungu cyangwa abagabo. Intanga zidasohotse zisubira mu mubiri cyangwa zigasohoka mu nzozi.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MYT-026: Wamenya ko umuntu afite indwara yandurira mu mibonano umurebye cyangwa umuhumuriza.

Si byo. Indwara nyinshi zandurira mu mibonano nta bimenyetso bigaragara cyangwa impumuro zigira. Uburyo bwonyine bwo kumenya ni ukwipimisha, kandi abakundana bombi bakwiye kwipimisha.

*risk: None; sources: STI*

### MYT-027: Gufata ibinini bibiri byihutirwa bikora neza kurushaho.

Si byo. Gufata ibinini byihutirwa birenze ntibitanga uburinzi bwinshi kandi bishobora gutera ingaruka nyinshi. Fata urugero rwategetswe, vuba bishoboka nyuma y'imibonano idakingiye.

*risk: None; sources: EC*

### MYT-028: Agapira gashobora gutuma ukuboko kugagara.

Si byo. Agapira gashyirwa munsi y'uruhu gusa kandi ntikagira ingaruka ku myakura, imitsi cyangwa uko ukuboko kunyeganyega. Bamwe bagira ibara rito cyangwa uburibwe buke iminsi mike nyuma yo kugashyirwamo.

*risk: None; sources: FPH*

### MYT-029: Ibinyobwa bikonje cyangwa amazi akonje mu gihe cy'imihango bitera ubugumba.

Si byo. Ibinyobwa bikonje n'amazi ntibigira ingaruka ku mihango cyangwa ku bushobozi bwo kubyara. Nywa icyo wisanzuyeho.

*risk: None; sources: MEN*

### MYT-030: Ntushobora gutwita ukoze imibonano uhagaze.

Si byo. Gutwita bishobora kubaho uko mwaba muteye kose. Intanga zigenda ubwazo, uko mwaba muteye kose.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### MYT-031: Imiti gakondo ishobora gutuma umuntu agukunda.

Si byo. Nta muti, ikinyobwa cyangwa impigi byatuma umuntu agukunda. Urukundo rukura binyuze mu kubahana, kwizerana n'amahitamo. Bimwe muri ibi bicuruzwa bishobora no kwangiza ubuzima bwawe.

*risk: None; sources: TBC*

### MYT-032: Imibonano ituma abahungu bakura kandi bakagira imbaraga.

Si byo. Uburebure n'imbaraga biterwa n'uruhererekane, ubugimbi, imirire myiza, gusinzira n'imyitozo, si imibonano.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

## Uburenganzira na serivisi (Rights and services)

### SRV-014: Ubuzima bw'imyororokere ni iki?

Ubuzima bw'imyororokere n'imibonano mpuzabitsina ni ukumera neza mu mubiri, mu mutwe no mu mibanire ku bijyanye n'imibonano, gukura no kubyara. Burimo kumenya umubiri wawe, ubwangavu n'imihango, gufata ibyemezo bitekanye, kwirinda inda zitateganyijwe, virusi itera SIDA n'izindi ndwara zandurira mu mibonano, imibanire irimo kubahana no kwemera, no kubaho nta hohoterwa. Mu Rwanda, urubyiruko rufite uburenganzira bwo guhabwa amakuru nyayo na serivisi zigenewe urubyiruko, mu ibanga, ku bigo nderabuzima no muri serivisi z'urubyiruko.

*risk: None; sources: AP; LAW25*

### SRV-001: Itegeko rishya ry'ubuzima mu Rwanda rivuze iki ku rubyiruko?

Itegeko N° 026/2025 rigenga serivisi z'ubuvuzi, ritangira gukurikizwa kuva ku wa 18 Nzeri 2025, ryamanuye imyaka yo kwiyemerera serivisi z'ubuzima, harimo n'iz'imyororokere, kuva kuri 18 kugeza kuri 15. Ibi bivuze ko abafite imyaka 15 kuzamura bashobora guhabwa serivisi nko kuboneza urubyaro no kwirinda virusi itera SIDA batabanje gusaba uruhushya rw'ababyeyi cyangwa undi muntu.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko ku kigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: LAW25*

### SRV-002: Umukozi w'ubuzima azabibwira ababyeyi banjye cyangwa ishuri?

Abakozi b'ubuzima bagomba kugira ibanga amakuru y'ubuzima bwawe. Niba ufite imyaka 15 cyangwa irenga, ushobora guhabwa serivisi z'ubuzima bw'imyororokere ku giti cyawe. Bitandukanye ni igihe uri mu kaga, urugero nk'ihohoterwa, aho bashobora kwitabaza inzego zishinzwe kurinda kugira ngo bakurinde. Ushobora kubaza umukozi w'ubuzima ibizasangizwa abandi mbere yo kuvuga.

*risk: None; sources: LAW25*

### SRV-003: Serivisi z'urubyiruko (youth corner) ni iki?

Youth corner, cyangwa serivisi zinogeye urubyiruko, ni ahantu ku bigo nderabuzima byinshi no mu bigo by'urubyiruko urubyiruko rushobora kubona amakuru na serivisi nk'ubujyanama, kuboneza urubyaro, udukingirizo, kwipimisha virusi itera SIDA no kuvurwa indwara zandurira mu mibonano, bitangwa n'abakozi bahuguriwe kwakira urubyiruko nta kurucira urubanza.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'urubyiruko ku kigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: TBC*

### SRV-004: Ni izihe nimero z'ubufasha nahamagara mu Rwanda?

Ibyihutirwa (polisi, akaga): 112. Umurongo w'ubuzima, harimo n'ubufasha ku buzima bwo mu mutwe: 114, ni ubuntu. Isange One Stop Centre ku ihohoterwa: 3029, ni ubuntu. Umurongo wo kurwanya ihohoterwa: 3512. Umurongo wo gufasha abana bari munsi y'imyaka 18: 116, ni ubuntu. RIB: 166.

*risk: None; sources: HL*

### SRV-005: Umukozi w'ubuzima yancira urubanza mbajije udukingirizo. Nakora iki?

Ufite uburenganzira bwo kwitabwaho mu cyubahiro no mu ibanga. Ibyo wahuye nabyo ntibikwiye. Ushobora gusaba kwakirwa n'undi mukozi, ukajya kuri serivisi z'urubyiruko, cyangwa ugakoresha farumasi. Ushobora kandi kubimenyesha ubuyobozi bw'ikigo nderabuzima.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: TBC*

### SRV-006: Urubyiruko rufite ubumuga rufite uburenganzira bumwe ku buzima bw'imyororokere?

Yego. Urubyiruko rufite ubumuga rufite uburenganzira bumwe bwo kubona amakuru, gukundana, kuboneza urubyaro, kuvurwa virusi itera SIDA no kurindwa ihohoterwa. Serivisi z'ubuzima zigomba kugerwaho n'abantu bose, harimo ubufasha mu itumanaho. Niba serivisi zitakugeraho, saba ubufasha cyangwa ubimenyeshe ikigo cy'ubuzima.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: VAW*

### SRV-007: Ubwisungane mu kwivuza (Mutuelle) bwishyura serivisi z'ubuzima bw'imyororokere?

Ubwisungane mu kwivuza (Mutuelle de santé) bwishyura serivisi nyinshi mu bigo nderabuzima no mu bitaro bya Leta. Serivisi nyinshi zo kuboneza urubyaro, iza virusi itera SIDA n'izo kwipimisha inda nazo zitangirwa ku buntu mu bigo bya Leta. Baza ku kigo nderabuzima ibyo wishyurirwa.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: TBC*

### SRV-008: Umujyanama w'ubuzima ashobora kumpa uburyo bwo kuboneza urubyaro?

Yego. Mu Rwanda, abajyanama b'ubuzima bo mu mudugudu bashobora gutanga uburyo bumwe, nk'ibinini, inshinge n'udukingirizo, n'inama, kandi bakakohereza ku kigo nderabuzima ku bundi buryo nk'agapira cyangwa IUD.

*Aho wajya: Umujyanama w'ubuzima; risk: None; sources: FP*

### SRV-009: Hari ushobora kumpatira kwipimisha virusi itera SIDA?

Oya. Kwipimisha virusi itera SIDA bigomba kuba ku bushake no ku bwemerere bwawe, biherekejwe n'ubujyanama, kandi igisubizo cyawe ni ibanga. Niba ufite imyaka 15 cyangwa irenga, ushobora kwiyemerera kwipimisha.

*risk: None; sources: LAW25; WHO*

### SRV-010: Intyoza ikora iki n'ibyo nandika?

Intyoza ikoresha ubutumwa bwawe gusa kugira ngo ibone igisubizo cyiza. Ntikeneye izina ryawe, kandi ntisangiza abandi ikiganiro cyawe. Niba ufunguye uburyo bwo kwibuka, ingingo wabajije zonyine ni zo zibikwa kuri telefone yawe, si ubutumwa bwawe.

*risk: None; sources: MH*

### SRV-011: Nabona he ubufasha nijoro nyuma y'ihohoterwa?

Isange One Stop Centre zifatanye n'ibitaro; niba ibiro bya Isange bifunze nijoro, jya muri serivisi z'ibyihutirwa z'ibitaro, aho abakozi bashobora kuguha ubuvuzi bwihuse harimo imiti irinda virusi itera SIDA no gutwita. Ushobora no guhamagara 112.

*Aho wajya: Serivisi z'ibyihutirwa ku bitaro; 112; risk: None; sources: ISG*

### SRV-012: Nakwiga he byinshi ku buzima bw'imyororokere ku ishuri?

Integanyanyigisho z'amashuri mu Rwanda zirimo inyigisho ku buzima bw'imyororokere mu masomo ya siyansi n'imibereho y'abaturage, zigaruka ku mikurire, imibanire, uburenganzira no kwirinda virusi itera SIDA. Ushobora no kubaza umuforomo w'ishuri, umurezi (patron cyangwa matron), cyangwa serivisi z'urubyiruko ku kigo nderabuzima.

*risk: None; sources: TBC*

### SRV-013: Ndi munsi y'imyaka 15. Nshobora kubona ubufasha ku kigo nderabuzima?

Yego. Ibigo nderabuzima bifasha n'abana. Kuri serivisi zimwe, umukozi w'ubuzima ashobora kwitabaza umubyeyi cyangwa umuntu mukuru wizeye, ariko niba uri mu kaga cyangwa uhohoterwa, bazagufasha kandi bakurinde. Ushobora no guhamagara umurongo w'ubuntu wo gufasha abana 116.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: LAW25*

## Ubuzima bw'abahungu (Boys' health)

### BOY-001: Igitsina cyanjye gihagarara nta mpamvu, ndetse no mu ishuri. Ni ibisanzwe?

Yego. Mu bugimbi, imisemburo ituma igitsina gihagarara kenshi kandi rimwe na rimwe nta mpamvu, n'iyo udatekereza ku mibonano. Ni ibisanzwe kandi akenshi bishira mu minota mike. Gutekereza ku kindi kintu, cyangwa kwicara ushyize igitabo ku bibero, bifasha.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### BOY-002: Mfite uburibwe butunguranye kandi bukabije mu gasabo k'intanga (ubugabo).

Uburibwe butunguranye kandi bukabije mu gasabo k'intanga bushobora guterwa no kwihotora kwako, bikeneye kubagwa mu masaha make kugira ngo karokorwe. Jya ku bitaro cyangwa ku kigo nderabuzima bikwegereye ako kanya, cyangwa uhamagare 112. Ntutegereze ngo urebe niba bishira.

*Aho wajya: Ibitaro nonaha; 112; risk: Emergency; sources: WHO*

### BOY-003: Kimwe mu bice by'agasabo k'intanga kiruta ikindi cyangwa kimanutse cyane. Ni ibisanzwe?

Yego. Birasanzwe cyane ko intanga imwe iba nini gato cyangwa ikamanuka kurusha indi. Jya kwa muganga niba ubonye ikibyimba gishya, kubyimba, uburemere cyangwa uburibwe.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### BOY-004: Nsohora vuba cyane mu mibonano. Nakora iki?

Gusohora vuba birasanzwe, cyane cyane ku basore no mu rukundo rushya, kandi akenshi bigenda bigabanuka uko igihe kigenda n'impungenge zigabanuka. Kugenda buhoro, gutuza no kuganira n'umukunzi wawe bifasha. Niba bikomeje kuguhangayikisha, umukozi w'ubuzima yakugira inama mu ibanga.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: WHO*

### BOY-005: Rimwe na rimwe igitsina cyanjye ntigihagarara cyangwa ntigitinda guhagarara. Hari ikibazo mfite?

Ibi bibaho ku bagabo benshi igihe runaka kandi akenshi biterwa n'umunaniro w'ibitekerezo, ubwoba, umunaniro, inzoga cyangwa ibiyobyabwenge. Niba bibaho kenshi, umukozi w'ubuzima yasuzuma izindi mpamvu. Irinde kugura ibinini ku bacuruzi batemewe, kuko bishobora guteza akaga.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: WHO*

### BOY-006: Hari amashyira cyangwa ibindi bisohoka mu gitsina cyanjye.

Ibisohoka mu gitsina cy'umugabo, akenshi biherekejwe no kokera iyo anyara, akenshi ni ikimenyetso cy'indwara yandurira mu mibonano nk'imitezi cyangwa chlamydia. Jya ku kigo nderabuzima wipimishe kandi uvurwe, wirinde imibonano kugeza uvuwe, kandi umukunzi wawe nawe agomba kuvurwa.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Low; sources: STI*

### BOY-007: Nasukura nte igitsina cyanjye niba ntarasiramuwe?

Karaba buri munsi ukoresheje amazi meza. Subiza inyuma uruhu rutwikiriye umutwe w'igitsina buhoro buhoro aho rugarukira nta kubabara, woze munsi yarwo, wiyuhagire hanyuma urusubize aho rwari ruri. Ntukarukurure ku ngufu. Niba rufunze cyane, rubabaza cyangwa rubyimba, jya kwa muganga.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

### BOY-008: Ni byiza ko abahungu barira cyangwa bavuga ku byiyumvo byabo?

Yego. Abahungu n'abagabo nabo bagira ibyiyumvo, kandi kubivuga ni ikimenyetso cy'imbaraga, si intege nke. Kubika byose imbere bishobora gutera umunaniro, uburakari cyangwa agahinda. Vugana n'inshuti, uwo mu muryango, mwarimu cyangwa umujyanama, cyangwa uhamagare umurongo w'ubuntu 114.

*risk: None; sources: MH*

### BOY-009: Nisuzuma nte amabya yanjye?

Rimwe mu kwezi, nyuma yo kwiyuhagira amazi ashyushye, kungura buhoro buri bya hagati y'intoki. Menya uko asanzwe ameze. Niba ubonye ikibyimba gikomeye, kubyimba cyangwa impinduka mu ngano, jya kwa muganga. Ibibyimba byinshi si kanseri, ariko ni byiza gusuzuma hakiri kare.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; risk: None; sources: WHO*

### BOY-010: Ni ibisanzwe ko intanga zisa ukundi rimwe na rimwe?

Yego. Amasohoro akenshi aba yera cyangwa asa n'ikijuju kandi ashobora kuba afatanye cyangwa yoroshye bitewe n'umunsi. Jya kwa muganga niba atukura cyangwa asa n'ikigina, anuka nabi, cyangwa aherekejwe n'uburibwe, kuko bishobora kuba ikimenyetso cy'indwara.

*risk: None; sources: PUB*

## Inzoga n'ibiyobyabwenge (Alcohol and drugs)

### DRG-001: Inzoga zigira izihe ngaruka ku byemezo bijyanye n'imibonano?

Inzoga zituma bigorana gutekereza neza, bityo abantu bakaba bakora imibonano batateguye, bakirengagiza agakingirizo, cyangwa bagahatirwa. Umuntu wasinze cyane ntashobora kwemera imibonano. Niba unywa, guma hamwe n'inshuti wizeye kandi utegure uko utaha mu mutekano.

*risk: None; sources: MH; RBCD*

### DRG-002: Urumogi rwemewe mu Rwanda?

Oya. Gukoresha, kugura, kugurisha cyangwa guhinga urumogi ku giti cyawe ni icyaha mu Rwanda kandi bishobora gutuma ufungwa. Urumogi rushobora kandi kwangiza kwibuka, kwiga n'ubuzima bwo mu mutwe, cyane cyane ku rubyiruko ubwonko bwarwo bukiri gukura.

*risk: None; sources: LAW*

### DRG-003: Kunywa urumogi bigira izihe ngaruka ku rubyiruko?

Urumogi rushobora kugira ingaruka ku kwibuka, ku kwita ku bintu no ku musaruro w'amashuri, kandi rushobora gutera guhangayika, kugira ubwoba budasobanutse cyangwa uburwayi bwo mu mutwe bukomeye ku rubyiruko rumwe. Kurukoresha kenshi bishobora gutera kurubatwa. Niba ushaka kurureka cyangwa kugabanya, umukozi w'ubuzima cyangwa umurongo w'ubuntu 114 bagufasha.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; 114; risk: Low; sources: RBCD; MH*

### DRG-004: Inshuti yanjye yanyoye inzoga nyinshi kandi ntibyuka.

Ibi bishobora kuba uburozi buturuka ku nzoga, bushobora kwica. Hamagara 112 nonaha. Ntumusige wenyine. Muryamishe ku ruhande kugira ngo atanigwa naruka, umushyushye, kandi ntumuhe izindi nzoga, ikawa cyangwa ngo umwoze amazi akonje.

*Aho wajya: 112; ikigo nderabuzima; risk: Emergency; sources: WHO*

### DRG-005: Ndashaka kureka inzoga ariko sinshobora.

Gushaka kureka ni intambwe ya mbere y'ingenzi. Gerageza kwishyiriraho intego nto, kwirinda ahantu n'inshuti bijyanye no kunywa, no kubwira umuntu wizeye. Ibigo nderabuzima mu Rwanda bivura ibibazo by'inzoga, kandi ushobora guhamagara umurongo w'ubuntu 114 ukabona ubufasha. Ntugomba kubikora wenyine.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; 114; risk: Low; sources: RBCD*

### DRG-006: Nakwirinda nte ko banshyirira ikintu mu cyo kunywa?

Hora ufite icyo kunywa cyawe, wemere ibyo kunywa uhawe n'abantu wizeye cyangwa ibyo ubona bifungurwa, kandi ntusige icyo kunywa ahantu wenyine. Guma hamwe n'inshuti. Niba wumvise utunguranye usinze cyane, ufite urujijo cyangwa ibitotsi, bwira inshuti ako kanya kandi ujye ahantu hatekanye. Niba ukeka ko wahohotewe, jya kuri Isange (3029) vuba bishoboka.

*Aho wajya: Isange 3029 niba wahohotewe; risk: Low; sources: VAW*

### DRG-007: Kunywa itabi, vape cyangwa shisha bigira ingaruka?

Yego. Itabi na nikotine birabata kandi byangiza ibihaha, umutima n'imikurire, kandi itabi ryongera ibyago bya kanseri. Vape nazo zirimo nikotine n'ibindi binyabutabire. Shisha irabujijwe mu Rwanda. Uko utangira kare, ni ko bigorana kureka.

*risk: None; sources: WHO*

### DRG-008: Nabona he ubufasha ku kubatwa n'ibiyobyabwenge mu Rwanda?

Tangirira ku kigo nderabuzima: serivisi z'ubuzima bwo mu mutwe zihari zishobora gusuzuma no kuvura ibibazo by'ibiyobyabwenge n'inzoga, kandi zikakohereza ahandi bibaye ngombwa. Ushobora no guhamagara umurongo wa RBC 114 w'ubuntu. Ibigo ngororamuco bitanga ubujyanama n'ubuvuzi bw'ihungabana. Gusaba ubufasha ni intambwe ikomeye.

*Aho wajya: Ikigo nderabuzima; 114; risk: Low; sources: RBCD*
